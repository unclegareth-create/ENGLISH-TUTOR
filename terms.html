Lesson Bookings & Payment

All private tutoring sessions are booked in advance. Trial lessons are booked and paid for through Stripe via our Cal.com calendar integration. Multi-lesson packages (5 or 10 lessons) and recurring lessons can be paid via Stripe, PayPal, or Wise in GBP (£). Where applicable, your bank or card provider converts into your local currency at their standard exchange rate.

Package Validity & Expiry

Multi-lesson packages (5-lesson and 10-lesson packs) remain valid for 6 months from the date of purchase. This allows students ample flexibility to pause for holidays, work deadlines, or exam study periods while maintaining momentum.

Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Schedules change and unexpected events occur. Lessons may be rescheduled or cancelled at no charge provided at least 12 hours' advance notice is given via email or calendar reschedule link. If notice is provided with less than 12 hours before the scheduled start time, or in the case of a missed session without notice, the session will be charged in full.

Package Refunds (Pro-Rata)

If you purchase a 5- or 10-lesson package and your circumstances change so that you cannot complete the remaining hours, unused lessons can be refunded pro-rata. The refund amount will be calculated based on the lessons already delivered (billed at the standard single-lesson rate of £45) subtracted from the total package price paid, with any remaining balance returned to your original payment method.

Statutory Cancellation Rights (UK Consumer Contracts Regulations)

In accordance with the UK Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, you have a statutory right to cancel a distance contract within 14 calendar days from the day of purchase without giving any reason, and receive a full refund.

If you book and request a lesson to take place within this 14-day statutory cooling-off period, you expressly request the commencement of services during the cancellation period. If you subsequently exercise your right to cancel, you will remain liable for the proportionate cost of any tutoring services already delivered prior to cancellation.

Punctuality

Lessons start promptly at the scheduled time. If a student arrives late, the lesson will still finish at the scheduled end time to avoid impacting subsequent students. If the tutor is delayed due to technical issues, the lost time will be added to the session or credited to a future lesson.
