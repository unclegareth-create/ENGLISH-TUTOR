4. Statutory 14-Day Cancellation Rights for Services (UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013)

In accordance with the UK Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, UK/EU consumers have a statutory right to cancel a distance service contract within 14 calendar days from the day the contract is entered into without giving any reason.

If you book a live lesson or submit work for a 24-hour Asynchronous Audit to be delivered within this 14-day cooling-off period, you expressly request that performance of the service begins before the end of the 14-day cancellation period. Under Regulation 36, if you subsequently exercise your right to cancel before the service is fully performed, you remain liable to pay for the proportionate value of the service delivered up to the time you notify us of cancellation. Once a single lesson or Asynchronous Audit has been fully performed within the 14-day period at your express request, the right to cancel that completed service ceases.

5. Digital Content & Self-Paced Courses (Regulation 37 Cooling-Off Waiver)

When you purchase immediate access to digital content not supplied on a tangible medium—specifically Dark Academia: C1/C2 English Through Classic Gothic Stories (£24) and its accompanying downloadable study compendium—delivery of the digital content (via instant browser unlock link and course passkey) begins immediately upon completion of checkout.

Under Regulation 37 of the UK Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, consumers normally have a 14-day cooling-off period to cancel digital content purchases unless delivery begins immediately with your prior express consent. By ticking the consent box or proceeding to checkout for immediate course access, you:

  • Expressly consent to the immediate supply and unlocking of the digital course lessons and study materials before the 14-day statutory cancellation period expires; and
  • Acknowledge that once immediate access to or download of the digital content has begun, your 14-day statutory right to cancel is lost.

Your Statutory Quality Rights: Waiving the 14-day cooling-off period for immediate access does not affect your statutory rights under the UK Consumer Rights Act 2015, which requires all digital content to be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose, and as described. Furthermore, if you purchase the course and have not yet used your passkey or unlocked the paid lessons, or if you experience any technical issue accessing your passkey, please email Gareth directly within 14 days and a full refund or immediate replacement access link will be provided.
