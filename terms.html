4. Statutory 14-Day Cancellation Rights for Services (UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013)

In accordance with the UK Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, UK/EU consumers have a statutory right to cancel a distance service contract within 14 calendar days from the day the contract is entered into without giving any reason.

If you book a live lesson or submit work for 48-hour Written Essay Feedback to be delivered within this 14-day cooling-off period, you expressly request that performance of the service begins before the end of the 14-day cancellation period. Under Regulation 36, if you subsequently exercise your right to cancel before the service is fully performed, you remain liable to pay for the proportionate value of the service delivered up to the time you notify me of cancellation. Once a single lesson or Written Essay Feedback review has been fully performed within the 14-day period at your express request, the right to cancel that completed service ceases.
