Exact Analytical Identities Verified in This Laboratory

1. Coupled Competitive ODEs & Washout Duration \(\Delta t_{\mathrm{wash}}\)

Sensitive cells \(S(t)\) and resistant cells \(R(t)\) compete within carrying capacity \(K\) under asymmetric competition coefficient \(\alpha_{RS} > 1\) and plasma drug concentration \(C(t)\):

$$\frac{dS}{dt} = r_S S\left(1 - \frac{S + R}{K}\right) - k_d C(t)S, \qquad \frac{dR}{dt} = r_R R\left(1 - \frac{\alpha_{RS} S + R}{K}\right)$$

After dosing stops at \(t_{\mathrm{off}}\) with peak concentration \(C_{\max}\), first-order clearance \(C(t) = C_{\max}e^{-k_e(t - t_{\mathrm{off}})}\) maintains net negative sensitive growth until plasma concentration falls to the critical balance threshold \(C_{\mathrm{crit}} = \frac{r_S}{k_d}(1 - S_{\min}/K)\), producing the exact washout lag \(\Delta t_{\mathrm{wash}} = \frac{1}{k_e}\ln\!\left(\frac{C_{\max}}{C_{\mathrm{crit}}}\right)\).

2. Poisson-Bernoulli Safe-Stopping Threshold \(S_{\mathrm{off}}^*\)

Integrating the non-autonomous Bernoulli equation during washout with total kill impulse \(\Lambda_0 = k_d C_{\max}/k_e\) and propagator \(\Phi(t) = \exp\!\big(r_S t - \Lambda_0(1 - e^{-k_e t})\big)\) yields the exact expectation over \(M \sim \operatorname{Poisson}(\Lambda_0)\):

$$\mathcal{I}(t) = \mathbb{E}_{M\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\Lambda_0)}\!\left[\frac{e^{(r_S - M k_e)t} - 1}{r_S - M k_e}\right], \qquad S_{\mathrm{off}}^* = \frac{S_{\min}}{\Phi(\Delta t_{\mathrm{wash}}) - \frac{r_S S_{\min}}{K}\mathcal{I}(\Delta t_{\mathrm{wash}})}$$

Terminating dosing early at \(S(t_{\mathrm{off}}) = S_{\mathrm{off}}^*\) guarantees that the post-cessation trajectory touches the Darwinian containment floor tangentially (\(\min_{t \ge t_{\mathrm{off}}} S(t) = S_{\min}\)) without breaching it.

3. Sub-MIC Mutant Selection Window (MSW) & Escape Hazard

During drug elimination, the sub-MIC selection window spans the interval where \(C(t)\) drops below the resistant minimum inhibitory threshold while remaining above the sensitive threshold \(C_{\mathrm{crit}}\). De novo and standing escape hazard over cycle \([0, T]\) obeys:

$$H_{\mathrm{esc}} = \mu \int_0^T r_S S(t)\,\max\!\left(0, \, 1 - \frac{\alpha_{RS} S(t) + R(t)}{K}\right) dt, \qquad P_{\mathrm{esc}} = 1 - \exp(-H_{\mathrm{esc}})$$

Whenever uncompensated washout drives \(S(t) < S_{\min} = K/\alpha_{RS}\), the competitive suppression factor \(1 - \alpha_{RS}S(t)/K\) becomes positive throughout the washout trough, opening an evolutionary escape window.

4. Bivariate Cross-Washout & Quadratic Elimination Ratio

Under sequential switching between primary agent \(C_1(t) = C_{1,\max}e^{-k_{e,1}t}\) and secondary agent \(C_2(t) = C_{2,\max}(1 - e^{-k_{e,2}t})\), the normalized dual-exposure overlap integral \(\mathcal{I}_{i\to j} = \int_0^\infty (C_i/C_{i,\max})(C_j/C_{j,\max})\,dt\) satisfies:

$$\frac{\mathcal{I}_{2\to 1}}{\mathcal{I}_{1\to 2}} = \left(\frac{k_{e,1}}{k_{e,2}}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{t_{1/2,2}}{t_{1/2,1}}\right)^2, \qquad k_d\langle C_1\rangle_T < r_S\left(1 - \frac{1}{\alpha_{RS}} + \frac{\mu_2}{\alpha_{RS}}\right)$$

Pairing a fast-clearing primary agent with a slow-clearing secondary agent produces quadratic overlap asymmetry, while orthogonal collateral bursts (\(\mu_2 > 0\)) widen the Volterra speed limit into a double-bind extinction wedge.
