Case File: Operation Clandestine Infiltration

From Gareth's C2 Psychometrics & Passives Masterclass. Formulate 4 institutional arrangements made by the operative using ONLY have/had + object + V3 or got + object + to/V3:

1. Security Clearance: "The agent **had his diplomatic passport renewed** under an alias."
2. Surveillance Evasion: "They **got the telecom provider to divert** encrypted transmissions."
3. Forensic Audit: "The director **had the financial archives independently scrutinized**."
Pedagogical Rationale:
In C2 Proficiency writing and IELTS Speaking Part 3, causatives establish institutional objectivity, omitting personal pronouns while maintaining decisive agency.
