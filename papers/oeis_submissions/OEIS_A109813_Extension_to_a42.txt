3. COMMENT / ALGORITHM FIELD (Paste into COMMENTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All six unknown terms ('?') in _Franklin T. Adams-Watters_'s Sep 25 2006 comment (at n = 29, 31, 35, 37, 39, 41) can be computed in polynomial time with respect to the prime-signature counts by combining inclusion-exclusion over Hamiltonian paths with two structural reductions on the coprime graph G_n:
(1) Partition the active primes p <= n/2 (with p not dividing n) into small primes p <= sqrt(n) (together with p = 2) and large primes q > sqrt(n). Every vertex in V_n = {1 <= k <= n : gcd(k,n) = 1} is divisible by at most one large prime q > sqrt(n), so the vertices divisible by q form disjoint clusters C_q.
(2) Any subset chosen from C_q whose elements have pairwise non-disjoint small-prime bitmasks (or size <= 1) introduces no new forbidden edges beyond the small primes and is absorbed directly into the small-mask multiplicity state s[mu]; similarly, 2-element and 3-element subsets on {q, 2q, 3q} are evolved via shared pair/triple counters, reducing the walk matrix at each inclusion-exclusion leaf to an 8-state sum-over-subsets (SOS) transform over the bitmasks of {2, 3, 5}. - _Gareth Montgomery_, Oct 29 2026
