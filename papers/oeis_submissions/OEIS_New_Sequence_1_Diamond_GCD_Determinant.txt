%N AXXXXXX Determinant of the n X n arithmetic diamond matrix A_n with entries A_n(i,j) = gcd(i+j, |i-j|) for 1 <= i, j <= n.
%C AXXXXXX Whereas H. J. S. Smith's classical GCD matrix G_n = [gcd(i,j)]_{1<=i,j<=n} has determinant Product_{k=1..n} phi(k) (A001088), coupling additive sum and difference inside the greatest common divisor produces the identity gcd(i+j, |i-j|) = gcd(i,j) * (1 + [v_2(i) == v_2(j)]), where v_2(k) = A007814(k) is the 2-adic valuation.
%C AXXXXXX Under unit lower-triangular congruence over the odd divisors, A_n decomposes into a direct sum of dyadic Kac-Murdock-Szego (KMS) Toeplitz blocks D_V(a,b) = 2^{min(a,b)}(1 + delta_{a,b}) (0 <= a,b <= V), whose normalized determinants are governed by the Pisot sequence P(2,7) (A020727), equivalently the interleaved Pell numbers (A000129) and companion Pell numbers (A001333).
%C AXXXXXX Even though consecutive ratios a(n)/a(n-1) are non-integral rationals introducing and later cancelling companion Pell prime factors (7 at n=2..3 cancelled at n=4; 41 at n=8..15 cancelled at n=16; 239 at n=32..63 cancelled at n=64), Smith's determinant A001088(n) = Product_{k=1..n} phi(k) divides a(n) for every n >= 1.
%F AXXXXXX a(n) = Product_{m=1..n, m odd} ( phi(m)^(V_m + 1) * 2^(floor(V_m^2 / 2)) * W(V_m + 2) ), where V_m = floor(log_2(n/m)) and W(k) = A000129(k) (k even) or A001333(k) (k odd).
%F AXXXXXX a(n) = A001088(n) * Product_{m=1..n, m odd} A020727(floor(log_2(n/m))).
%F AXXXXXX For n = 2^v * m with m odd and v = A007814(n), a(n)/a(n-1) = phi(m) * A020727(v) * 2^(v-1) / A020727(v-1) for v >= 1, and a(n)/a(n-1) = 2 * phi(m) for v = 0.
%e AXXXXXX For n = 2, A_2 = [[2, 1], [1, 4]], so a(2) = 2*4 - 1*1 = 7.
%e AXXXXXX For n = 3, A_3 = [[2, 1, 2], [1, 4, 1], [2, 1, 6]], so a(3) = 28.
