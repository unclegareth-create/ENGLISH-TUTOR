What is this?!

Audio in, audio out. Dessa is a true speech-to-speech architecture where everything comes from human conversation. Because it is full-duplex, it listens and talks at the same time, just as you do in a real Cambridge Speaking exam or boardroom meeting. It knows when to cut in and when to backchannel (“yeah!”, “got it!”, “ooh”) to show it is right there with you.

It natively hears and speaks emotion, sarcasm, laughter, a frustrated sigh, or a subtle shift in certainty. Run speech through a text transcript first and all of that acoustic ink is wiped clean—which is why standard voice bots feel stilted, slow, and exhausting.

This is crazy!

Text-only LLMs need an astronomical amount of written tokens. Spoken language carries meaning in dimensions that never appear on a page: accents, pauses, breathing, irony, inflection, and nuclear stress.

Speech never repeats. Say a sentence twice, exactly the same way, and the acoustic waveform still comes out different. Text is identical no matter who types it. People learn to talk so naturally that we forget how rich the signal is—and why mastering spoken English requires training your ear and rhythm, not memorising vocabulary lists.
