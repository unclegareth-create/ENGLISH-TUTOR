Frequently Asked Questions

Can you work remotely with our existing software?

Yes. I can log securely into your cloud system (BrightPay, Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, or your bespoke agency portal) with restricted permissions to process timesheets and reconcile records.

How quickly do you resolve disputed invoices?

Disputed invoices are reviewed within 24 hours of notification. I cross-reference signed timesheets and rate cards to deliver a clear reconciliation summary to your client's accounts payable team.

Do you replace our chartered accountant?

No, I work alongside your year-end accountant. By keeping your weekly payroll, VAT, and ledgers immaculate throughout the year, your accountant spends fewer billable hours cleaning up messy books in January.
