Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about switching accountants, fees, and cloud setup.

How difficult is it to switch from my current accountant?
It is completely hassle-free. You simply send a short email to your previous accountant stating you are moving, and we handle the rest. We send the official professional clearance letter, request your previous year's working papers, and transfer your HMRC agent authorisations seamlessly with zero disruption to your filing schedule.
Are there any setup fees or hidden charges?
No. We believe in transparent, fixed-fee monthly subscriptions. There are zero onboarding fees. If your business has catch-up bookkeeping or overdue accounts from previous years, we will review the scope and provide a fixed quote before starting any work.
When should a Sole Trader switch to a Limited Company?
Generally, when your net profits consistently exceed £35,000–£40,000 per year, trading through a Limited Company becomes significantly more tax efficient due to the difference between personal income tax/NICs and the small profits Corporation Tax rate. It also provides limited liability protection and commercial credibility. We can run a side-by-side comparison for you during our discovery call.
What software do you recommend and support?
We are official certified partners with Xero and QuickBooks. For clients on our Limited Company Growth package, a full Xero subscription is included in your monthly fee. We also set you up with Dext (receipt scanning) so you can photograph receipts on your phone and throw the paper away.
Can you help international founders or UK expats?
Yes. We regularly advise international entrepreneurs establishing UK corporate entities, handling cross-border VAT on digital services, and navigating UK Statutory Residence rules and double-taxation treaties.
