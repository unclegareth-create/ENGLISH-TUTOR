1-Page C1/C2 Red-Pen Checklist

By Gareth Montgomery · 19 years teaching (15 at Belfast Met) · Keep beside you while drafting or checking essays and Part 4 scripts.

Formatted for 1-Page A4 Print

1. Five C1 Structural Shifts (Use 3 or 4 per Essay — Never Force All Five)

Restraint beats rare thesaurus words

2. The Error vs Style Filter (Protect Your Error-Free Sentence Ratio)

Genuine Errors (Must Fix Before Handing In)

  • Uncountable nouns pluralised: never add -s to information, advice, equipment, research, evidence, staff, infrastructure, or physical damage.
  • Missing / extra articles: check plural generalisations (most universities, not the universities) and singular countable nouns.
  • Preposition & verb traps: explain to me (not explain me), depend on (not of), discuss [topic] (no about), arrive in/at (not to).
  • Conditional timing clashes: keep would out of standard if-clauses (If councils funded...).

Style Choices (Grammatical — Not an Error)

  • Discussed if vs debated whether: both are grammatical English; changing if to whether is only a register preference.
  • Most of the species vs most species: grammatical when referring to a bounded group in context.
  • Pose a challenge to vs for: both prepositions are standard academic collocations.
  • Clear split infinitives (to quickly check) & opening with However / Yet: acceptable usage. Never let an automated checker scare you into rewriting sound prose.

3. My 4-Step Cambridge Part 4 Elimination Check

Strict limit: 3–6 words (CAE/CPE) · Contractions = 2 words
Step 1 · Strike Out Echoes

Cross out every word in Sentence 1 already printed in Sentence 2 to isolate the exact missing meaning.

Step 2 · Tense Harmony

Identify Sentence 1’s timeframe (Past Simple, Past Perfect, modal past) and carry that exact tense into the gap.

Step 3 · Voice Shift

Check if Sentence 2 opens with the object or a noun phrase — signalling an Active ↔ Passive flip or nominalisation.

Step 4 · Lock Key Word

Slot into the C1 frame in 3–6 words. Keep the capitalised key word 100% unchanged (no -s/-ed/-ing).
