Cambridge C1 Advanced & C2 Proficiency • IELTS Band 7.5–9.0 12 Self-Paced Lessons (20 Mins Each)

Learn C1/C2 English Through Classic Gothic Stories

Great novelists use the exact same grammar patterns that Cambridge and IELTS examiners reward: negative inversion, participle clauses, cleft sentences, and past modal deductions. In these 12 focused lessons, you'll read famous scenes from four classic stories, pick up high-value modern vocabulary, and practice using those structures naturally in real-world essays, reviews, and articles.

How Each 20-Minute Lesson Works

When you read 19th-century fiction in English, you don't want to end up sounding like a Victorian ghost in your exam essays. Each lesson follows four practical steps:

Step 1 • Read

The Original Scene

Read a short, memorable excerpt from the original novel with the key grammar patterns highlighted in context.

Step 2 • Filter

Words to Keep vs. Leave

Separate useful Modern C1/C2 Words (great for essays and speaking) from Old-Fashioned Words you only need to recognize when reading.

Step 3 • Practice

Grammar & Part 4 Transforms

Learn how the grammar pattern works in plain English, then test yourself with Cambridge Use of English Part 4 Key Word Transformations.

Step 4 • Transfer

Use It in Modern Writing

See how the exact same sentence pattern works naturally in a modern C1/C2 or IELTS essay, book review, or article.
