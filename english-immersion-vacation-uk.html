Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about airports, UK visitor rules, accommodation, and how to prepare online first.

When will the first Stay & Learn Residencies take place?
The Northern Ireland Stay & Learn Residency is a future flagship addition to Gareth English, planned for strictly limited seasonal windows (Spring, Summer, and Early Autumn). Because we only host one solo student or duo at a time, priority invitations will go first to current 1-to-1 online students and members of the Priority Waitlist below.
Which airports serve Belfast and Northern Ireland?
Northern Ireland is exceptionally well connected:
  • George Best Belfast City Airport (BHD): 10 minutes from central Belfast; direct flights from London Heathrow, London City, Amsterdam, and UK hubs.
  • Belfast International Airport (BFS): 25 minutes from Belfast; direct flights across Europe.
  • Dublin Airport (DUB): 90 minutes south of Belfast via direct 24/7 express coach straight to Belfast Grand Central Station—ideal for direct long-haul flights from North America, the Middle East, and major European capitals.
Do I need a UK Student Visa for a 4- to 10-day immersion residency?
Under UK Immigration Rules (Appendix V: Visitor), international visitors can undertake short recreational or English language courses of up to 30 days (and up to 6 months) under standard visitor entry without needing a Student Visa. Non-visa nationals (including EU/EEA, Swiss, US, Canadian, Japanese, and South Korean citizens) simply obtain a digital UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) online before travelling (including when travelling into Northern Ireland via Dublin Airport).
Who is eligible to attend, and can I bring my partner or a colleague?
Residencies are designed exclusively for adults aged 18+ at intermediate-to-advanced levels (CEFR B1+ to C2). You are very welcome to attend as a 2-to-1 Duo with a partner, spouse, or colleague of a similar English level (saving ~26% per person), or to bring a non-studying partner when selecting Model A (Boutique Partner Hotel/B&B).
