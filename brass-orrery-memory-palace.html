Planetary Ring VII: The 4-Step Part 4 Astrolabe Calibration

Elevate the 6 Courtyard Foundations to C1/C2 Cambridge Part 4 mastery using Gareth's 4-Step Elimination & Deconstruction Strategy: (1) Strike out echoes, (2) Inspect tense harmony, (3) Check voice shift, and (4) Fit the 3–6 word gap with the unchanged keyword.

1. The stone viaduct was not only engineered for steam trains, but it also withstood severe earthquakes.
KEYWORD: ONLY (MISSING) 0 WORDS (3–6 REQ)
Ring I Agreement & Inversion: Past Simple Passive (was engineered) Formula: Not only + was + Subject + V3
Not engineered for steam trains, but it also withstood severe earthquakes.
Cambridge Model: only was (1 mark) // the (stone) viaduct (1 mark)
Fronting Not only inverts the singular passive auxiliary was before the subject.
2. I am certain that the chief horologist knew about the gear misalignment.
KEYWORD: BOUND (MISSING) 0 WORDS (3–6 REQ)
Ring II State Verb & Aspect: State verb (knew) → Perfect Infinitive (to have known) Formula: BE + bound to + have + V3
The chief horologist about the gear misalignment.
Cambridge Model: was/is bound to (1 mark) // have known (1 mark)
Because know is a non-continuous state verb in the past, it takes the simple perfect infinitive to have known.
3. The observatory last calibrated the brass astrolabe five years ago.
KEYWORD: BEEN (MISSING) 0 WORDS (3–6 REQ)
Ring III Chasm Bridge: Past Simple (ago) → Present Perfect Bridge (has been... since) Formula: It + has been + [period] + since + Past Simple
It the observatory last calibrated the brass astrolabe.
Cambridge Model: has been five years (1 mark) // since (1 mark)
Bridges the finished past event (calibrated) to the present moment using It has been [duration] since.
4. The astronomers had barely begun their lunar observation when the storm shutters slammed shut.
KEYWORD: SCARCELY (MISSING) 0 WORDS (3–6 REQ)
Ring IV Interrupted Sequence: Past Perfect (had barely begun) → Inverted Past Perfect Formula: Scarcely + had + Subject + V3 ... when
their lunar observation when the storm shutters slammed shut.
Cambridge Model: Scarcely had (1 mark) // the astronomers begun (1 mark)
Scarcely had + subject + V3 pairs with when + Past Simple interruption.
5. The apprentices had no idea how much precision the pendulum clock required.
KEYWORD: LITTLE (MISSING) 0 WORDS (3–6 REQ)
Ring V Quantifier Inversion: Past Simple (had no idea) → Negative Inversion (Little did... know) Formula: Little + did + Subject + V1 (know/realise)
how much precision the pendulum clock required.
Cambridge Model: Little did (1 mark) // the apprentices know/realise (1 mark)
Fronting restrictive Little triggers did + subject + bare infinitive.
6. The curator blamed the apprentice for damaging the brass sundial.
KEYWORD: FAULT (MISSING) 0 WORDS (3–6 REQ)
Ring VI Voice & Frame Shift: Active Gerund (damaging) → Passive Clause (was damaged) Formula: it was + [person's] + fault (that)
According to the curator, the brass sundial was damaged.
Cambridge Model: it was the apprentice's (1 mark) // fault (that) (1 mark)
Notice the active-to-passive shift at the end of Sentence 2 (was damaged), requiring a finite that-clause after it was the apprentice's fault.

Memory Palace Rule Summary & Self-Test Drawer

Review all 7 Planetary Rings at a glance and test your active recall before reveal.

Ring I · Agreement Balance Bridge

Everyone / Nobody / Each / Advice = 1 Sealed Singular Box (takes is / was / has / verb+s). Ignore prepositional distractors between subject and verb.

Ring II · Stone Pillar vs Turbine

Present Simple = permanent facts & schedules. am/is/are + -ing = temporary action in progress now. State verbs (agree, know, belong) never spin into -ing.

Ring III · Present Perfect Drawbridge

have/has + V3 = Bridge DOWN to NOW (for, since, so far, present result). Past Simple (V2) = Bridge UP for finished past timestamps (yesterday, ago, in 2020).

Ring IV · Past Continuous Interrupted

was/were + -ing = longer background wave. Past Simple (V2) = sudden interrupting action or sequential chain (Step 1 → Step 2).

Ring V · Articles & Mass Vats

a/an = 1 of many / jobs. the = shared spotlight. Ø Zero Article = plurals/concepts in general & uncountable mass nouns (advice, information, traffic).

Ring VI · AT (0D) · ON (2D) · IN (3D)

AT = clock points & pins (at 6:30, at the door). ON = surfaces, walkable decks & days (on Friday, on the train). IN = 3D rooms, cars, months & years.
