The Part 4 Deconstructor: "Strike Out the Echoes"

In nineteen years of preparing candidates for Cambridge C1 Advanced—fifteen of those lecturing in Belfast—I have watched more strong students tie themselves in knots over Part 4 than over any other section of the paper. Most candidates stare at the whole of Sentence 1, panic, and start guessing vaguely similar sentences from memory. Toss that habit out right off the bat.

Part 4 is not a creative writing exercise; in the cold light of day, it is a two-mark accounting puzzle. Every item carries two marks split cleanly down the middle (1 mark // 1 mark). If you cross out the words in Sentence 1 that are already echoed in Sentence 2, the exact piece of meaning you still owe the examiner stands out plain as day. Follow my four-step routine below on every single question.

Gareth Montgomery · CELTA (2007), BA University of Stirling · 1-to-1 Cambridge & IELTS Coaching
Step 1 · Isolate

Strike Out the Echoes

Cross out every word in Sentence 1 that already appears in Sentence 2. What remains in amber is your exact semantic deficit—nothing more, nothing less.

Step 2 · Anchor

Inspect Tense Harmony

Check the verb tense in Sentence 1. If the original is past simple, present perfect, or modal past, your transformed gap must honour that exact timeframe.

Step 3 · Pivot

Check Voice & Word Order

Look at what moved to the front of Sentence 2. Did the object become the subject (passive shift), or did a negative adverbial land first (inversion)?

Step 4 · Lock In

Solve & Guard the Keyword

Fit the remaining meaning into 3 to 6 words around the unchanged keyword. Altering the keyword—even by a single letter—scores zero marks.
