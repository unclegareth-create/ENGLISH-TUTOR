## Why Standard UK Salary Calculators Fail Between £100,000 and £125,140
1. **The 62% (rUK) / 69.5% (Scotland) Personal Allowance Taper (`ITA 2007 s.35 & s.58`)**:
   - Between £100,000 and £125,140 of Adjusted Net Income (`ANI`), £1 of Personal Allowance (£12,570) is withdrawn for every £2 earned.
   - In England/Wales/NI (`rUK`), this combines with 40% Higher Rate Tax + 20% PA withdrawal + 2% Employee NI = **62.0% marginal deduction** (or **71.0%** with Plan 1/2/4/5 Student Loan, **77.0%** with Postgrad Loan).
   - In Scotland, the 45% Advanced Rate combines with 22.5% PA withdrawal + 2% Employee NI = **69.5% marginal deduction** (**78.5%** with Student Loan).
2. **The £100,000.00 Childcare Cliff (`Childcare Act 2016` & `SI 2015/522`)**:
   - Exceeding £100,000.00 of Adjusted Net Income by even £0.01 disqualifies the household from 30 Hours Funded Childcare and Tax-Free Childcare (worth ~£8,500/year per preschool child).
   - For a parent earning £110,000 with 2 nursery-age children, unoptimized take-home + benefits is **£12,000+ lower** than if they sacrificed £4,500 into a pension or SIPP to bring ANI to £100,000.
3. **The 15% Employer NI Pass-Back Multiplier (`NIC Act 2025` & `ITEPA 2003 s.308`)**:
   - From April 2025/2026, Employer Class 1 National Insurance is **15%** above £5,000.
   - When an employee sacrifices £10,000 inside the £100k-£125,140 band:
     - Employee net take-home cash drops by only **£3,800** (rUK) or **£3,050** (Scotland).
     - If the employer passes back 50%-100% of their 15% Employer NI saving, **£10,750 to £11,500** lands in the employee's pension pot—an instant **+182.9% to +277.0% risk-free return on cash sacrificed** before counting any restored childcare subsidy.
