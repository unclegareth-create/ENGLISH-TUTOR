Stage 2 · The Belfast Desk Routine

My 3-Step Gap Eliminator

Never read up to the blank and grab the first word that sounds familiar. Run these three quiet checks in order on every single gap.

01

Read the full clause (ignore topic nouns)

Read from capital letter to full stop. Unfamiliar nouns like masonry or botanists never decide a Part 2 blank. Strip the sentence back to its verbs, conjunctions, and punctuation so you see the exact clause boundary.

Clause frame: [Subject] + [Verb] + ___ + [Complement]
02

Diagnose the exact slot BEFORE thinking of a word

Name the grammatical job first. Is the gap linking two clauses, completing a fixed preposition, inverting an auxiliary, or quantifying a noun? Naming the slot cuts fifty wild guesses down to a shortlist of three.

Slot family -> 3-word shortlist -> 1 exact match
03

Check one-word discipline & agreement

Write strictly ONE word. Avoid contractions (don't and isn't count as two words in Cambridge marking). Before moving on, check left for prepositions and right for tense, singular/plural agreement, and logic.

1 word only · No contractions · Check left & right
