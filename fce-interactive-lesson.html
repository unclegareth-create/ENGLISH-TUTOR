Interactive Lesson: FCE Exam Review

Preparing for the Cambridge B2 First exam? Click through this interactive presentation I designed to help my students review essential grammar, vocabulary, and exam strategies. It's the perfect way to test your knowledge before exam day.

How to use this interactive lesson

Click the Play button below to start the presentation. Use the arrows to navigate forward and backward through the topics. I recommend taking notes on any new vocabulary or structures you discover along the way.

Why interactive practice works for B2 First

The FCE exam tests not just your knowledge of English, but your ability to apply it accurately under pressure. By actively engaging with the material in this presentation, you are reinforcing your memory much more effectively than passive reading.

Key areas to focus on:

  • Use of English Part 4 (Key Word Transformations): Look out for phrasal verbs and fixed expressions.
  • Speaking Part 2 (Long Turn): Notice the language used for comparing and contrasting.
  • Writing: Pay attention to linking words and formal versus informal register.
