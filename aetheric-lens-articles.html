How to Choose the Right Article in 2 Seconds

Never memorise 40 textbook rules—just ask these 2 questions in order:
Question 1 (Ask This First!)

Do we BOTH know which exact one(s) I mean?

• YES → Use THE immediately! (Doesn’t matter if it’s singular, plural, or uncountable!)
• NO → Go to Question 2 →

→
Question 2 (If It’s General or New)

Can I count “ONE single...” of it right here?

• YES (It’s 1 countable thing) → Use A / AN.
• NO (It’s plural or uncountable) → Use NO ARTICLE (Ø).
