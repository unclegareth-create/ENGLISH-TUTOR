Theorem 2.2 (Universal Jordan-Pisot Power Diamond Formula)

For any integer exponent \(r \ge 1\), the 2-adic resonance \(\gcd(i^r+j^r, |i^r-j^r|) = \gcd(i,j)^r(1 + \delta_{v_2(i), v_2(j)})\) and odd-divisor Jordan totient \(J_r(m)\) Möbius congruence reduce \(A_n^{(r)} = [\gcd(i^r+j^r, |i^r-j^r|)]_{1\le i,j\le n}\) to dyadic Kac-Murdock-Szegő (KMS) Toeplitz blocks at coupling \(\rho_r = 2^{-r/2}\):

$$\det\big[\gcd(i^r+j^r, \, |i^r-j^r|)\big]_{1\le i,j\le n} = \prod_{\substack{m=1 \\ m\text{ odd}}}^{n} J_r(m)^{V_m + 1} \, 2^{r V_m(V_m - 1)/2} \, \Delta_{V_m}^{(r)}, \quad V_m = \lfloor \log_2(n/m)\rfloor$$

Here \(\Delta_V^{(r)} = 2^{r+1}\Delta_{V-1}^{(r)} - 2^r\Delta_{V-2}^{(r)}\) (\(\Delta_0^{(r)} = 2, \Delta_1^{(r)} = 2^{r+2}-1\)) satisfies the universal Cassini identity \((\Delta_V^{(r)})^2 - \Delta_{V+1}^{(r)}\Delta_{V-1}^{(r)} = 2^{r(V-1)}\), proving that \(\Delta_V^{(r)}\) is the exact Pisot sequence \(T(2, 2^{r+2}-1)\) for every \(r \ge 1\): OEIS A020727 (\(T(2,7)\) for \(r=1\)) and OEIS A022026 (\(T(2,15): 2, 15, 112, 836, 6240, \dots\) for \(r=2\)).

Theorem 3.1 (Shifted Smith \(2^{n-2}\) Law & \(n=14\) Phase Transition)

For the additively shifted Smith matrix \(G_n^{(k)} = [\gcd(k+i, k+j)]_{1\le i,j\le n}\), parity-difference basis annihilation of \(\phi(1)=\phi(2)=1\) and \(\mathbb{F}_2\) rank-nullity across prime powers \(p^r \le n-1\) with \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\) establish for all \(n \ge 2\) and \(k \in \mathbb{Z}\):

$$v_2\big(\det G_n^{(k)}\big) \ge (n - 2) + \max\Big(0, \, \lfloor n/2\rfloor - 2 - \pi_{3,4}^*(n-1)\Big)$$

This proves why \(\min_{k\ge 0} v_2(\det G_n^{(k)}) = n - 2\) holds for \(2 \le n \le 13\), and fails for the first time at \(n = 14\), where \(\lfloor 14/2\rfloor - 2 = 5 > 4 = |\{3, 7, 9, 11\}| = \pi_{3,4}^*(13)\).

Theorem 2.1 (Poisson-Bernoulli Washout & Safe-Stopping Law)

When dosing ceases at \(t=0\) with elimination rate \(\gamma = \ln 2 / t_{1/2}\) and total washout kill impulse \(\Lambda_0 = k_d C_{\mathrm{off}}/\gamma\), the nonlinear crowding integral \(\mathcal{I}(t) = \int_0^t \Phi(\tau)\,d\tau\) is the exact expectation over \(M \sim \operatorname{Poisson}(\Lambda_0)\):

$$\mathcal{I}(t) = \mathbb{E}_{M\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\Lambda_0)}\!\left[\frac{e^{(r_S - M\gamma)t}-1}{r_S - M\gamma}\right], \qquad S_{\mathrm{stop}}^* = \frac{S_{\mathrm{floor}}}{\Phi(t^*) - \frac{r_S S_{\mathrm{floor}}}{K}\mathcal{I}(t^*)}$$

Inverting the Möbius relation at the nadir time \(t^* = \gamma^{-1}\ln\!\big(k_d C_{\mathrm{off}} / [r_S(1 - S_{\mathrm{floor}}/K)]\big)\) yields the exact stopping threshold \(S_{\mathrm{stop}}^*\) required to guarantee \(\min_{t\ge 0} S(t) = S_{\mathrm{floor}}\).
