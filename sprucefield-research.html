The Dyadic Kac-Murdock-Szegő Spectrum of Arithmetic Diamond Matrices and the \(n=14\) Phase Transition in Shifted Smith Determinants

Resolving two additive deformations of H. J. S. Smith's 1875 determinant \(\det[\gcd(i,j)] = \prod_{k=1}^n \phi(k)\) that break factor-closure by coupling multiplicative divisibility with additive operations.

📄 Read PDF TeX Source
Theorem 2.2 (Arithmetic Diamond Master Formula)

By the 2-adic resonance identity \(\gcd(i+j, |i-j|) = \gcd(i,j)(1 + \delta_{v_2(i), v_2(j)})\), odd-divisor Möbius congruence reduces \(A_n = [\gcd(i+j, |i-j|)]_{1\le i,j\le n}\) to a direct sum of dyadic Kac-Murdock-Szegő (KMS) Toeplitz blocks at the critical coupling \(\rho = 1/\sqrt{2}\):

$$\det\big[\gcd(i+j, \, |i-j|)\big]_{1\le i,j\le n} = \left(\prod_{k=1}^n \phi(k)\right) \prod_{\substack{m=1 \\ m\text{ odd}}}^{n} \Delta_{\lfloor \log_2(n/m)\rfloor}$$

Here \(\Delta_V = 4\Delta_{V-1} - 2\Delta_{V-2}\) (\(\Delta_0 = 2, \Delta_1 = 7\)) is the Pisot sequence \(P(2,7)\) (OEIS A020727: \(2, 7, 24, 82, 280, 956, \dots\)), equivalently the interleaved Pell (A000129) and companion Pell (A001333) numbers scaled by \(2^{\lfloor V/2\rfloor}\).

Theorem 3.1 (Shifted Smith \(2^{n-2}\) Law & \(n=14\) Phase Transition)

For the additively shifted Smith matrix \(G_n^{(k)} = [\gcd(k+i, k+j)]_{1\le i,j\le n}\), parity-difference basis annihilation of \(\phi(1)=\phi(2)=1\) and \(\mathbb{F}_2\) rank-nullity across prime powers \(p^r \le n-1\) with \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\) establish for all \(n \ge 2\) and \(k \in \mathbb{Z}\):

$$v_2\big(\det G_n^{(k)}\big) \ge (n - 2) + \max\Big(0, \, \lfloor n/2\rfloor - 2 - \pi_{3,4}^*(n-1)\Big)$$

This proves why \(\min_{k\ge 0} v_2(\det G_n^{(k)}) = n - 2\) holds for \(2 \le n \le 13\), and fails for the first time at \(n = 14\), where \(\lfloor 14/2\rfloor - 2 = 5 > 4 = |\{3, 7, 9, 11\}| = \pi_{3,4}^*(13)\).

2. OEIS A392191 · Spezia's Circulant Conjecture Raw Proof & Code (.txt)

Resolves Stefano Spezia's January 2026 conjecture for the minimum determinant \(a(n)\) of an \(n \times n\) circulant matrix whose rows are permutations of \((0, 1, \dots, n-1)\), and establishes \(a(12) = -75986875920384\) and \(a(13) = 1014\):

$$a(n) = \begin{cases} -\operatorname{A392190}(n), & n \text{ even}, \\ \frac{p-1}{2}p^2 = \operatorname{A084367}\!\big(\frac{p-1}{2}\big), & n = p \text{ odd prime}, \\ 0, & n = 1 \text{ or } n \ge 9 \text{ odd composite}. \end{cases}$$

Mechanism: For odd composite \(n = dm \ge 9\), Kotzig's \(3 \times d\) equal-column-sum magic rectangle annihilates the eigenvalue \(L_m = 0\). For odd prime \(p\), cyclotomic ideal divisibility \((1-\zeta_p) \mid L_1\) forces \(\frac{p-1}{2}p^2 \mid \det(C)\), attained by the pyramidal permutation \(c = (0, 2, 4, \dots, p-1, p-2, \dots, 3, 1)\).

3. OEIS A227477 · Lipschitz Quaternion Group Exponent Raw Proof & Code (.txt)

Resolves the 2013 `[hard,more]` open problem for the group exponent \(a(n)\) of the unit group \(\mathbb{H}(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^\times\) of reduced Lipschitz quaternions modulo \(n = 2^{e_0}\prod_{i=1}^r p_i^{e_i}\):

$$a(n) = \operatorname{lcm}\!\left(2^{e_0}, \, \operatorname{lcm}_{1\le i\le r} p_i^{e_i}(p_i^2 - 1)\right)$$

Mechanism: For odd primes \(p\), \(\mathbb{H}(\mathbb{Z}/p^e\mathbb{Z})^\times \cong \operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/p^e\mathbb{Z})\) has exponent \(p^e(p^2-1)\) via a Singer cycle of order \(p^2-1\) times a \(p\)-unipotent lift. For \(p=2\), every odd-norm quaternion satisfies \(q^2 \in 1 + 2\mathbb{H}(\mathbb{Z}/2^e\mathbb{Z})\), which has exponent \(2^{e-1}\) by 2-adic filtration induction, yielding \(a(2^e) = 2^e\).

4. OEIS A236864 · Symmetric \(2 \times 2\) Matrix Power Sums Raw Proof & Code (.txt)

Establishes the exact number-theoretic characterization of all integers \(n\) for which the \(n\)-th power sum of symmetric \(2 \times 2\) matrices over \(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}\) does not vanish modulo \(n\):

$$\sum_{M \in \operatorname{Sym}_2(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})} M^n \not\equiv 0 \pmod n \iff \exists \text{ prime } p \text{ such that } p \parallel n \text{ and } p(p^2 - 1) \mid n$$

Mechanism: Spectral summation over \(\mathbb{F}_p\) and \(\mathbb{F}_{p^2}\) shows that \(S(p, n) \not\equiv 0 \pmod p\) iff \((p^2-1) \mid n\). Whenever \(p^2 \mid n\) (\(e \ge 2\)), first-order Hensel fiber summation over the \(p^3\) lifts \(M = M_0 + p^{e-1}D\) vanishes identically modulo \(p^e\).

5. OEIS A339948 · 2-Adic Quaternion Ring Classification Raw Proof & Code (.txt)

Classifies the isomorphism classes of generalized quaternion rings \(H_{\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}}(a,b)\) for \(a,b \in \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}\), correcting a 1-argument (`phi[2^s]`) vs 2-argument (`phi[2, s]`) Mathematica pattern-matching bug that corrupted all even composite terms and \(a(32)\) in the published entry:

$$a\!\left(2^{s_0}\prod_{i=1}^r p_i^{s_i}\right) = a(2^{s_0}) \prod_{i=1}^r (2s_i^2 + 2), \quad \big(a(2^s)\big)_{s=1}^7 = (1, \, 7, \, 16, \, 32, \, 56, \, 90, \, 138)$$

Mechanism: Over \(\mathbb{Z}/16\mathbb{Z}\), \(2 \cdot 8 \equiv 0 \pmod{16}\) unlocks non-orthogonal \(\operatorname{GL}_4(\mathbb{Z}/16\mathbb{Z})\) basis shifts such as \((X,Y) = (i+j, j)\) that merge \(H(4,8) \cong H(12,8)\) and \(H(2,4) \cong H(2,12) \cong H(6,4) \cong H(6,12)\), yielding \(a(16) = 32\) and \(a(32) = 56\).

Exact Pharmacokinetic Washout Compensation and Volterra Cycle Conservation in Evolutionary Cancer Containment

Solving the coupled nonlinear logistic-washout Bernoulli differential equation in exact closed form via a Poisson expectation \(M \sim \operatorname{Poisson}(k_d C_{\mathrm{off}}/\gamma)\) to prevent post-cessation washout breaches below the Darwinian containment floor \(S_{\mathrm{floor}} = K/\alpha\).

📄 Read PDF TeX Source
Theorem 2.1 (Poisson-Bernoulli Washout & Safe-Stopping Law)

When dosing ceases at \(t=0\) with elimination rate \(\gamma = \ln 2 / t_{1/2}\) and total washout kill impulse \(\Lambda_0 = k_d C_{\mathrm{off}}/\gamma\), the nonlinear crowding integral \(\mathcal{I}(t) = \int_0^t \Phi(\tau)\,d\tau\) is the exact expectation over \(M \sim \operatorname{Poisson}(\Lambda_0)\):

$$\mathcal{I}(t) = \mathbb{E}_{M\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\Lambda_0)}\!\left[\frac{e^{(r_S - M\gamma)t}-1}{r_S - M\gamma}\right], \qquad S_{\mathrm{stop}}^* = \frac{S_{\mathrm{floor}}}{\Phi(t^*) - \frac{r_S S_{\mathrm{floor}}}{K}\mathcal{I}(t^*)}$$

Inverting the Möbius relation at the nadir time \(t^* = \gamma^{-1}\ln\!\big(k_d C_{\mathrm{off}} / [r_S(1 - S_{\mathrm{floor}}/K)]\big)\) yields the exact stopping threshold \(S_{\mathrm{stop}}^*\) required to guarantee \(\min_{t\ge 0} S(t) = S_{\mathrm{floor}}\).

Theorems 3.1 & 3.2 (Volterra Speed Limit & Hysteresis Observer)

Logarithmic cycle integration over any periodic treatment-recovery cycle \([0,T]\) with \(S(T)=S(0)\) proves that resistant subclone regression (\(R(T) < R(0)\)) requires the evolutionary dosing speed limit:

$$k_d\langle C\rangle_T < r_S\left(1 - \frac{1}{\alpha}\right), \qquad \widehat{S}(N_{\mathrm{ref}}) = \frac{\dot{N}(t_{\uparrow}) - \dot{N}(t_{\downarrow})}{\kappa(C_{\downarrow}) - \kappa(C_{\uparrow})}$$

Moreover, subtracting downward on-drug slope \(\dot{N}(t_{\downarrow})\) from upward washout slope \(\dot{N}(t_{\uparrow})\) at equal total biomarker \(N_{\mathrm{ref}} = S + R\) cancels unknown growth functions \(F_S + F_R\) identically.

The Split-Mark Vector \((m_L, m_R)\) Poset Identifiability Theorem & Macro/Micro Decoupling

Proving why recording the ordered split-mark tuple \((m_L, m_R) \in \{0,1\}^2\) on Cambridge Part 4 transformations resolves all 10 hierarchical CEFR grammatical mastery states in \(N_{\min} = 3\) static items (\(2.30\) adaptive items), alongside my longitudinal corpus findings on Macro/Micro Decoupling and Closed-Class Syntactic Cadence (SCQS).

1. Poset Identifiability (\(N_{\min}=3\))

Each Cambridge Part 4 item carries a two-channel Q-matrix \((q_L, q_R)\) over 5 syntactic skills. While dichotomous scoring (`0/1`) conveys at most \(1\text{ bit/item}\) (requiring \(\ge 4\) items for 10 states) and scalar sum scoring (`0/1/2`) conflates \((1,0)\) with \((0,1)\), the ordered vector \((m_L, m_R)\) yields \(4^3 = 64\) response patterns and resolves all 10 Poset states into exact singletons (\(\log_2 10 \approx 3.3219\text{ bits}\)) in 3 items.

2. Macro/Micro Decoupling (\(p = 0.7258\))

Across 67 annotated learner sentences (\(N=47\) post-toolkit), candidates execute C1/C2 macro-frames (Wh-clefts, negative inversions, passive reporting) at 96.9% structural accuracy, yet L1 micro-slips (articles, uncountable plurals, prepositions) distribute orthogonally across C1 and non-C1 sentences (\(\chi^2 = 0.3113\), Fisher \(p = 0.7258\)).

3. Syntactic Cadence Score (\(\mathrm{AUC} = 1.000\))

My closed-class Syntactic Cohesion & Restraint Score (`SCQS`) penalises memorised template clichés and measures opener diversity alongside restrained C1 density (25% to 65% of sentences), separating authentic C1/C2 prose from formulaic template stuffing with \(\mathrm{AUC} = 1.000\) without relying on rare-word lookup tables.

4. Diachronic Conservation (1818 to 2026)

Across 15 paired 19th-century literary passages (Shelley 1818 to Doyle 1902) and 2026 C1/C2 transfers, finite subordination per sentence drops by \(-52.6\%\) (\(p = 0.0055\)) while nominal-participial compression rises \(+240.0\%\) (\(p = 0.0001\)), conserving total propositions per sentence (\(\mathrm{PPS}: 2.70 \to 2.92, p = 0.4778\)) as sentence length shrinks \(-30.2\%\).
