Part 3 Follow-Up • A.R.E.A. Builder Answer → Reason → Example → Alternative

“Some people claim that public funding should go only to practical services like transport and hospitals, rather than concerts, museums, or the arts. To what extent do you agree?”

C1 A.R.E.A. Model Response:
• Answer (Cleft): What many critics overlook is that cultural life and public wellbeing are closely connected.
• Reason (Passive Reporting): While hospitals and transport are obviously essential, access to music and public art is widely believed to reduce urban stress and bring communities together.
• Example: For instance, free summer concerts in city parks attract families who could never afford commercial ticket prices.
• Alternative (Inversion): Not only do cultural venues enrich daily life, but they also generate substantial local revenue through cafés and tourism.
Step 2 • C1 Structural Blueprints (12 Mins)

Targeted Grammar Formulas & Live Rewrite Workouts

Tailored directly to your syllabus: Cleft Sentences, Negative Inversions, Passive Reporting, and Timeline/Conditional Precision.

6 Live Drills
1. What-Cleft & It-Cleft Focus
What + Clause + BE + Noun / Clause It wasn't until + [Event] + that + [Past Simple]

Shifts emphasis to the exact cause or turning point without personal pronouns.

2. Negative Inversion
Not only + Aux + Subject + Verb, but... also... At no time / No sooner + Aux + Subject + Verb

When a negative adverbial opens the sentence, invert the auxiliary and subject just like a question.

3. Passive Reporting Verbs
It is widely argued / believed that + Clause Subject + BE + thought/said + to have + PP

Replaces informal "People say..." or "I think..." in CAE Part 1 Essays.

4. Inverted 3rd Conditional & Indirect Qs
Had + Subject + (not) + PP, ... would have + PP explain TO me + Question Word + Subject + Verb

Never put would in the if-clause; keep statement word order inside indirect questions.
