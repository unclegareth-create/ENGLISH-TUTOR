From Today's Desk: Cyprus, Stoicism & Natural Phrasing

New expressions, word families, and grammar patterns that came up naturally during our conversation today. Use Self-Test Mode to hide the definitions and test your active recall.

“It’s all go here in Cyprus!” Idiom • Preposition
Meaning: Everything is extremely busy and full of activity.
Note: Use in Cyprus (for the country/state) rather than on Cyprus (which is only used geologically for the physical island).
underwhelming Adjective
Meaning: Failing to impress; disappointing compared to the hype or expectation.
“The conference keynote was rather underwhelming compared to last year’s.”
stranded Adjective / Participle
Meaning: Left somewhere with no way to get home or move on.
“Zeno of Citium was left stranded in Athens after a shipwreck.”
a predicament C1 Noun
Meaning: An awkward, unpleasant, or difficult situation with no easy way out.
“Finding himself in a difficult financial predicament, he turned to philosophy.”
just → justice → unjust Word Family
just (adj): morally fair and right (a just society / a just decision).
justice (n): fairness; the legal/moral principle.
unjust (adj): unfair or morally wrong.
wandered into • became intrigued Narrative Verbs
wandered into a bookshop: walked in casually without a fixed plan.
became intrigued (by): had one's curiosity strongly sparked; became fascinated.
tolerance • intolerant • tolerate it when Grammar Pattern
Pattern: I can’t tolerate it when + [Subject + Verb]
Requires preparatory it before when (same rule as I hate it when... / I appreciate it when...).
colloquially Adverb
Meaning: In everyday, informal spoken language (as opposed to strict philosophical or technical meaning).
“Colloquially, ‘stoic’ means emotionless, which distorts the original Greek philosophy.”
logos • temperance • resolve • humane Stoic Lexis
logos: rational cosmic order / reason.
temperance: self-restraint and moderation.
humane treatment: compassionate, civilized treatment (note the -e: humane vs human).
resolve (n/v): firm determination to do something.
