Task 12 & Bonus C1 Grammar · Historical Coincidences & Conditionals

Famous Coincidences in History & C1 Transformations

Case 1 · 1898 vs. 1912
In 1898 Morgan Robertson published a novella called Futility, which imagined the sinking of a British ocean liner named Titan. Like the Titanic, it hit an iceberg in April, was considered ‘unsinkable’, and didn’t have enough lifeboats. The Titanic’s real sinking happened 14 years later.
Case 2 · The Ohio Twins
The Jim twins – identical twins separated at birth – were raised apart in Ohio, yet their lives turned out remarkably similar. Both were named James, both married women named Linda, and both had sons named James Allan. The twins also chose similar careers and became law enforcement officers. At age 39, they finally met.
  • Were you familiar with either story? Which of them do you find more remarkable? Why?
  • Do you think what happened in both stories are sheer coincidences, or might there be a plausible explanation for either of them?
  • Do you know of any stories similar to Futility, in which someone seemed to see something coming before it happened?
  • If the story wasn’t about identical twins, would people still read so much into the similarities in their lives? Why or why not?
