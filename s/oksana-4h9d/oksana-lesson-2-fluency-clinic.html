Step 4 · Spoken Fluency Builder: Complete the Thought Aloud

Choose 4 cards below. Start with the bold sentence frame and speak for 45–60 seconds on each one.

Confidence & Lexical Retrieval
Card 1 · Daily Energy

"What inspires me to get up in the morning is..."

Grammar target: What + Clause + is + Noun / Verb-ing. Talk about your morning routine before arriving at the clinic.

Card 2 · Professional Standards

"At work, the thing I value most in a colleague is..."

Grammar target: a colleague / the clinic / Ø hard work. Compare different attitudes to work and reliability.

Card 3 · Switching Off

"After a busy week at the dental practice, I usually suggest..."

Grammar target: suggest + Verb-ing (e.g. going for a meal, visiting a museum, listening to live jazz).

Card 4 · Past vs Present Habits

"When I first moved to Germany, I wasn't used to..., but now..."

Grammar target: be used to + Noun / Verb-ing vs used to + Base Verb.

Card 5 · Recommendations

"If a patient or friend asks me for advice about..."

Grammar target: Uncountable advice / information / progress (never an advice).

Card 6 · English Breakthrough

"The situation where I want to feel most confident speaking English is..."

Grammar target: feel confident + Verb-ing (travelling, speaking with international patients, socialising).
