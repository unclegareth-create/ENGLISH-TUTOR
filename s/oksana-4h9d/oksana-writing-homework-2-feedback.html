Four Patterns to Polish (Building on Homework #1)

Your voice and structure are great—here are the 4 habits to tidy up next:
Pattern 1 · Articles (a / an / the) From HW #1

Singular countable nouns need a / an / the, and another replaces the other when we mean “a different one”:

  • to the other country → to another country
  • with electric car → in an electric car
  • next day / a following day → the next day / the following day
  • totally different program → a totally different programme
Pattern 2 · Verb Patterns & Collocations From HW #1

Just like suggest + -ing from Homework #1, recommend also takes -ing (never to + verb):

  • recommend to visit → recommend visiting
  • give a try → give it a try
  • I came there 3 hours later → I arrived / got there 3 hours later
  • began our get to know from Champagne → broke the ice over Champagne
Pattern 3 · Past Tense & Agreement Storytelling

When reporting past conversations or thoughts, shift present verbs into the past, and watch singular vs plural:

  • asked me if I want → asked me if I wanted
  • no idea what is waiting → no idea what was waiting
  • one Ukrainian women → a Ukrainian woman
  • many hours concentration → many hours of concentration
Pattern 4 · Spelling, Capitals & Spacing Polish

A few quick spelling and punctuation clean-ups (plus programme from HW #1):

  • getaway (one word as a noun) · seafood (not seefood)
  • Versailles · Mont-Saint-Michel · D-Day sites
  • Baroque architecture (lowercase architecture) · French (capital F)
  • Put commas right after the word, with a space after: music, an explosion.
