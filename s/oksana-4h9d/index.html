🎙️ Active Voice Warm-Up • 2-Minute Long Turn Challenge

Describe a time when you helped someone with a problem.

You should say:

  • who you helped
  • what the problem was
  • how you helped this person
  • and explain how you felt afterwards.
1. The 1-Min Note Secret Write only 4 single trigger keywords down the margin. Never write full sentences.
2. The 3-Act Story Arc Past Context (30s) → Sensory Snapshot (45s) → Reflection / 'So What?' (45s).
3. Cambridge Anchor Bullet points are springboards, not an interrogation checklist. Deepen reflection!

10-Point Spoken Retrieval & Bochum Letter Repair Table

Built directly from your 'Hello Chris' Bochum Letter and our Lesson 1 Speaking Clinic: targeting articles (a / the / Ø), uncountable nouns, and natural English collocations. Zero homework writing needed - we can run through these aloud in 5 minutes!

1. Uncountable Nouns (No 'a')
What wonderful news! / great advice / good progress (never 'a')
2. Singular Countable Jobs/Places
It is a mining museum / there is a cafe / I am a dentist
3. Suggest + Verb-ing
I suggest driving / I suggest (that) we drive (never 'suggest to')
4. Lie Down vs. Lay Down
lie down (rest yourself) | take a seat (sit down)
