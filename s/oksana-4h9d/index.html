🎙️ Active Voice Warm-Up • 2-Minute Long Turn Challenge

Describe a patient communication challenge or modern technique in dentistry

  • What the dental procedure, preventive treatment, or restorative technique involves
  • How you explain complex oral health diagnoses to an anxious or sceptical patient
  • What empathy or communication strategy helps build long-term patient trust
  • And reflect on how dental technology has evolved to minimise patient discomfort
1. The 1-Min Note Secret Write only 4 single trigger keywords down the margin. Never write full sentences.
2. The 3-Act Story Arc Past Context (30s) → Sensory Snapshot (45s) → Reflection / 'So What?' (45s).
3. Cambridge Anchor Bullet points are springboards, not an interrogation checklist. Deepen reflection!

💡 C1 Structural Formula Workout

Interactive Reveal • Cambridge Model
Formula 01: Diplomatic Reassurance & Professional Explanations
While + [Procedure / Treatment] + may sound + [Intimidating], what it actually does is + [Gentle Clinical Benefit]

Drill Prompt: Explain a restorative procedure reassuringly: 'Root canal treatment sounds scary to patients, but modern anaesthesia makes it completely painless.'

→ "While endodontic therapy can sound intimidating to patients, modern local anaesthesia ensures the procedure is virtually painless, thereby preserving the natural tooth structure."
