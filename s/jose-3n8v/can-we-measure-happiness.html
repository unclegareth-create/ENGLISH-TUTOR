2-Minute Speaking Warm-Up: A Simple Thing That Makes You Happy

Describe an everyday activity or habit that reliably puts you in a good mood.

You should say:

  • what the activity or habit is
  • when and where you usually do it
  • how you discovered that it helps your mood

Tasks 4, 5 & 6 · The World Happiness Report

Can You Rate a Life on a Scale of 0 to 10?

The World Happiness Report looks at how happy people are with their lives in different countries around the world. The rankings are based mainly on people’s own evaluations of their lives, and they are asked to rate their lives on a scale of 0–10.

Task 4: Before Watching

  • Do you think this ranking shows the real situation? Why or why not?
  • What factors do you think researchers use to measure happiness?
  • Which countries would you expect to be at the top of the list? Where do you think Spain would be?
  • Is it actually possible to measure happiness objectively?

Part 6 · Structural Precision

Sentence Upgrades & Key Word Transformations

Complete the second sentence so that it has a similar meaning to the first sentence, using the word given in capitals. Do not change the word given. Use between three and six words.

1. When people compare themselves to others on social media, they feel less satisfied.
LESS
The more people compare themselves to others on social media, .
the less satisfied they feel (5 words) — Parallel double comparative ("The more..., the less...").
2. Even if someone has a very high salary, they can still feel lonely.
SPITE
A person can still feel lonely a very high salary.
in spite of having / in spite of earning (4 words) — "In spite of + Verb-ing / Noun".
3. Could you tell me how researchers measure happiness in different countries?
HOW
I’d like to know in different countries.
how researchers measure happiness (4 words) or how happiness is measured (4 words) — Indirect question + passive voice option.
4. Close relationships are much more important for happiness than expensive possessions.
WHAT
is close relationships rather than expensive possessions.
What matters most for happiness (5 words) — "What..." cleft sentence for emphasis.
5. People often forget to appreciate good health until they become ill.
GRANTED
People often until they become ill.
take good health for granted / take their health for granted (5 words) — Idiom: "take [something] for granted".
