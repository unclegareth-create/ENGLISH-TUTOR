MISSION 02 · THE CERTAINTY CODE

📐 Three Rules. Zero Confusion.

Click the matching certainty badge on each row below (+5 XP per correct row).

MUST HAVE + V3 → 99% sure it happened (strong evidence right in front of you)
MIGHT / COULD HAVE + V3 → 50% maybe it happened (one possible story)
CAN'T / COULDN'T HAVE + V3 → 99% impossible (evidence proves it did not happen)
⚠️ Two classic traps: never use mustn't have for guessing (use can't have), and always write have, never of!

MISSION 03 · BUG HUNT

🐞 Eight Corrupted Spy Messages

Each message has one grammar bug. Type your fix, then reveal the clean version.

1. ❌ He must went home early.
He must have gone home early. — Always use have + V3, never past simple went.
2. ❌ She mustn't have seen my text; she always replies!
She can't have seen my text; she always replies! — Use can't have for impossibility, never mustn't have.
3. ❌ You might of left your headphones on the bus.
You might have left your headphones on the bus. — Write have, never of.
4. ❌ They can't has opened the safe without the code.
They can't have opened the safe without the code. — Modal verbs always take base have, never has.
5. ❌ Someone must have took my charger.
Someone must have taken my charger. — The past participle (V3) of take is taken.
6. ❌ He can't have stole the watch; he was with me!
He can't have stolen the watch; he was with me! — The V3 form of steal is stolen.
7. ❌ Where is my pen? It might fell under the desk.
Where is my pen? It might have fallen under the desk. — Past guess needs have + V3.
8. ❌ Nobody can't have guessed that password!
Nobody could have guessed that password! — Avoid double negatives (nobody + can't).
