STAGE 4 • PAST MODALS OF DEDUCTION & INDEFINITE PRONOUNS

Crack the Alibi: Vault 4 Timeline & Suspects

Compare the 21:00–21:30 timeline against each suspect's alibi, complete the deduction drills, and lock in your accusation.

Structural Blueprint • Past Deduction & Indefinite Pronouns
must have + V3 (99% sure) Strong proof: “The pilot must have flown it through the vent.”
might / could have + V3 (possible) Open guess: “They could have hidden it on the roof.”
can't have + V3 (impossible) Ruled out by proof: “Maya can't have stolen it at 21:19.”
somebody / anybody / nobody / nowhere Use anybody after didn't; use nobody + singular verb; never double negatives.
