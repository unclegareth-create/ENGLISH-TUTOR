EXERCISES 7 & 8 • USEFUL LANGUAGE

Read the Dialogues & Sort the Phrases

Read each dialogue and decide where the conversation takes place: at a party, at home, or at a restaurant.

Dialogue A
a: Do you like mushrooms on pizza?
b: Yeah, I'm a big fan of mushrooms, but I can't stand olives.
a: No problem, I hate them too. Extra cheese?
b: Sounds perfect! If you call now, they'll deliver it in half an hour.
Dialogue B
a: What do you think of sushi?
b: I'm crazy about sushi! What about you?
a: I've tried it a few times, but raw fish is not my thing.
b: You can get sushi made with vegetables here. Let me ask the waiter for the menu.
Dialogue C
a: Look – I made a beautiful cake for this evening!
b: It looks really nice, but I'm not a big fan of chocolate I'm afraid.
a: No problem! How do you feel about ice cream? I think we have some.
b: I don't mind ice cream, but I'm still full after the huge dinner we had. Thanks anyway!

Asking About Preference

  • What do you think of…?
  • How do you feel about…?

Expressing Likes

  • I don't mind…
  • I'm a big fan of…
  • I'm crazy about…

Expressing Dislikes

  • I can't stand…
  • …is not my thing.
  • I'm not a big fan of…
