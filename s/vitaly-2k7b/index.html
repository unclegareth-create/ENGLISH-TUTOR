🎙️ Active Voice Warm-Up • 2-Minute Long Turn Challenge

Describe a useful gadget or invention that you use every day.

You should say:

  • what the gadget is
  • when you got it
  • what you use it for
  • and explain why it is so useful to you.
1. The 1-Min Note Secret Write only 4 single trigger keywords down the margin. Never write full sentences.
2. The 3-Act Story Arc Past Context (30s) → Sensory Snapshot (45s) → Reflection / 'So What?' (45s).
3. Cambridge Anchor Bullet points are springboards, not an interrogation checklist. Deepen reflection!

10-Point Mission Debrief: Indefinite Pronouns & Passive Voice

Built from Operation Nobody (Indefinite Pronouns Spy Mission) and How is Bubble Gum Made? (Passive Voice). Spot the grammar trap in each sentence and write your fix!

1. Everyone / Nobody + Singular Verb
Everyone IS ready / Nobody KNOWS (singular verb!)
2. No Double Negatives
I didn't see anybody OR I saw nobody (never 'didn't see nobody')
3. Pronoun + Adjective Order
something strange / somewhere quiet (adjective goes AFTER!)
4. Present & Past Passive
is/are + Past Participle (now) | was/were + Past Participle (past)
