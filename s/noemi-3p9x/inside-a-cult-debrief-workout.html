Grammar & Pronunciation Repairs

Spoken Accuracy & Silent Letters

  • it's quite scaring → it's quite scary scah-REE (predicative adjective, stress on syllable 1; never scaring).
  • they don't have found a sense → they haven't found (a sense of belonging/purpose). Negate have directly in Present Perfect.
  • subtle suh-til (/ˈsʌt.əl/) — the letter b is completely silent.
  • "Don't cults demand total obedience?" — negative question to invite agreement.
  • comforting / reassuring / easier • British spelling: organisation.
Causative Verbs • Personalised

Make, Let, Get & Be Made To

  • get + person + TO + verb (persuade/arrange): "I got Giovanni to fix the house!" • "Giovanni got me to go shopping!"
  • let + person + bare verb (allow, no to): "My parents didn't let me watch TV!"
  • make + person + bare verb (force): "The leader made new recruits give up their phones on day one."
  • be made + TO + verb (passive always keeps to): "New recruits were made to sign a contract they hadn't read."
Boundaries & Conversation

Life Maintenance & Friendships

  • "It's a dull day, but I put my make-up on and went out to do lots of jobs." → life maintenance (everyday adult errands).
  • "I've been thinking about other people's weaknesses recently." (Present Perfect Continuous for ongoing reflection).
  • an acquaintance (someone known slightly) • "You have a healthy self-image."
  • Transactional Analysis • "She's so weak and needy!" • "Tell her to talk to ChatGPT — so the friendship is over!"
  • "addicted to thrilling situations" (preposition to) • "your patience is exhausted" • "she should know better by now" • "she has never had a job".
Cults, Intensity & Idioms

Core Lexis & Bullshit Detector

  • relinquish control (hand over power) • brainwashing • a commune • utopian • charismatic.
  • grisly (adj., causing horror or revulsion, e.g. a grisly end).
  • sheer commitment = commitment alone (sheer intensifies pure commitment).
  • mass suicide — the Jonestown tragedy → "My friend doesn't believe in Covid or vaccines — he drank the Kool-Aid."
  • a bullshit detector — informal spoken idiom for a sharp instinct that spots manipulation or nonsense immediately.

1. Winning someone over early on with constant attention, flattery, and praise is called ____________ ____________.

love bombing — "Recruiters showered newcomers with attention and praise."

2. Members were expected to show unquestioning ____________ to the leader, while critics said the group used intimidation tactics.

loyalty — strong collocation: unquestioning loyalty (or total obedience from our scratchpad).

3. It took Maya years to ____________ ____________ the trauma of her time in the group.

get over — recover emotionally from a painful shock or experience.

4. When one leader makes every decision behind the scenes while followers do whatever they want, they have everyone wrapped around their ____________ ____________ (or they pull the ____________ / call the ____________).

little finger / strings / shots (also: have someone under your thumb).

5. When a group keeps members waiting with vague, empty promises while hiding its true motive, it is ____________ them ____________ with a hidden ____________.

stringing [them] along • agenda (related idioms: pull the wool over someone's eyes, a smoke screen).

6. When a close friend spots a clear warning sign, that is a ____________ ____________; ending all contact with a manipulative group means choosing to ____________ ____________.

red flag • cut ties (and leaving a situation for good is to walk away).

⚙️ Causative Verbs & Key Word Transformations

3–6 Words • Keyword Unchanged
Causative Verb Blueprint (Who Controls the Action?) make / let + [person] + [bare verb]  |  get + [person] + TO + [verb]  |  be made + TO + [verb]

Complete the second sentence so that it has a similar meaning to the first sentence, using 3 to 6 words including the key word in capitals. Do not change the key word.

Item 1 • Passive Causative MADE

The group forced new recruits to sign a contract they had not read.
New recruits _________________________________________ a contract they had not read.

were made to sign (4 words) — passive make always keeps to before the infinitive.
Item 2 • Persuasion Causative GOT

In the end, Noemi persuaded Giovanni to fix the house.
In the end, Noemi _________________________________________ the house.

got Giovanni to fix (4 words) — get + person + to + verb.
Item 3 • Permission Causative LET

My parents never allowed me to watch television on school nights.
My parents _________________________________________ television on school nights.

never let me watch (4 words; or did not / didn't let me watch) — bare infinitive without to.
Item 4 • Raising Concern HELP

I have clearly noticed that the group is taking all your money.
I _________________________________________ the group is taking all your money.

cannot help noticing (that) (3–4 words; or can't help noticing (that), 4–5 words).
Item 5 • Deception Idiom WOOL

The charismatic founder completely deceived his followers for years.
The charismatic founder _________________________________________ his followers' eyes for years.

pulled the wool over (4 words) — idiom: pull the wool over someone's eyes.
Item 6 • Scratchpad C1 Collocation RELINQUISH

Members gradually give up power over their daily choices to the leader.
Members gradually _________________________________________ their daily choices to the leader.

relinquish control over (3 words; or relinquish control of).

🗣️ Diplomatic Pushback & Tactful Softeners

Pages 7 & 9 • Raising Concern

Rephrase each blunt accusation so you can raise a concern with a worried friend without pushing them away, then compare with the model.

1. Blunt: "You've been brainwashed." I MIGHT BE WRONG

"I might be wrong, but it seems as though the group has started doing a lot of the thinking for you lately."

2. Blunt: "That group is taking all your money." I CAN'T HELP NOTICING

"I can't help noticing that the retreats seem to cost more every few weeks — are you comfortable with how much you've spent?"

3. Blunt: "You never see your family anymore." WHAT WOULD HAPPEN IF

"What would happen if you took one weekend off from the centre to catch up with your sister and close friends?"

4. Blunt: "Your leader is lying to you." HAVE YOU EVER WONDERED

"Have you ever wondered why members aren't allowed to ask where the group's donations actually go?"

5. Blunt: "You need to quit right now." I DON'T WANT TO INTERFERE

"I don't want to interfere, and I'm not judging you, but could we agree on taking a short break just to get some perspective?"
