🎙️ Active Voice Warm-Up • 2-Minute Long Turn Challenge

Describe a museum, gallery, or historical place you have visited.

You should say:

  • where it is
  • when and why you went there
  • what you saw there
  • and explain why you liked or disliked it.
1. The 1-Min Note Secret Write only 4 single trigger keywords down the margin. Never write full sentences.
2. The 3-Act Story Arc Past Context (30s) → Sensory Snapshot (45s) → Reflection / 'So What?' (45s).
3. Cambridge Anchor Bullet points are springboards, not an interrogation checklist. Deepen reflection!

Live Class Scratchpad & Sentence Repair Workout

Built from our live session on Different Seasons of Life, Aging & Priorities: focusing on prepositional verb patterns (explain to, look forward to + -ing), word formation (incapable of), and natural C1 expression.

1. Explain Pattern
explain [something] TO [someone] (never *explain me)
2. Word Formation & Gerund
incapable OF + -ing (never *uncapable to organise)
3. Phrasal Preposition
look forward to + -ing (gerund: getting older)
4. Nuance: Groggy vs Drowsy
groggy = dazed / sluggish | drowsy = sleepy (don't drive!)
