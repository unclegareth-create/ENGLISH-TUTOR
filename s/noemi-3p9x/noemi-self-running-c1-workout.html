Part B • 8-Gap C1 Open Cloze: “The Art of Everyday Curiosity”

Read the text below and think of the word which best fits each gap. Use only one word in each gap.

Children ask dozens of questions every single day, yet by the time we reach adulthood, we often take the world around us (1) granted. What keeps an adult mind sharp, (2) , is the willingness to wonder how ordinary things work. (3) only does genuine curiosity make conversations far more engaging, but it also helps us see eye to (4) with people whose backgrounds differ from our own. At first, asking thoughtful questions in a busy routine may not come naturally, but once you get used (5) slowing down and noticing small details, it quickly becomes second nature. In (6) of the constant distractions of modern life, setting aside even twenty minutes to read a book or visit a local gallery on the (7) of the moment can completely refresh your perspective. Little (8) we realise how much a single unexpected question can change the course of a conversation.
Cambridge Open Cloze Key: (1) for (take for granted) • (2) however / though (contrast linker) • (3) Not (Not only... but also) • (4) eye (see eye to eye) • (5) to (get used to + -ing) • (6) spite (In spite of) • (7) spur (on the spur of the moment) • (8) do (Little do we realise - negative inversion).
