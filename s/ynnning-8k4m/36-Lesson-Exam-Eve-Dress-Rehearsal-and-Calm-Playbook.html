Check 1 · Task 1 Past Simple Lock
In [past year] + Subject + Past Simple (reached / remained / spent)

“In 2020, commuter rail journeys reach a peak of 42 million, while average ticket prices remain steady at $18.”

👁️ Reveal Cambridge Model

“In 2020, commuter rail journeys reached a peak of 42 million, while average ticket prices remained steady at $18.”

Once a sentence anchors to a finished year like 2020, keep every verb in both halves in Past Simple.

Check 2 · Exact Numbers & Chart Agreement
The bar chart illustrates · Exact Number + million + Plural Noun (no -s, no of)

“The bar charts illustrates that the city attracted 12 millions of overnight visitors in 2015.”

👁️ Reveal Cambridge Model

“The bar chart illustrates that the city attracted 12 million overnight visitors in 2015.”

Match singular chart with illustrates, and drop -s and of whenever an exact number sits before million.

Check 3 · Indefinite Article (a / an)
a / an + Adjective + Singular Countable Noun

“Public green spaces create calmer urban atmosphere and offer safe outdoor area for local families.”

👁️ Reveal Cambridge Model

“Public green spaces create a calmer urban atmosphere and offer a safe outdoor area for local families.”

Singular countable nouns (atmosphere, area) always need a or an when modified by an adjective.

Check 4 · Fixed Verb & Noun Patterns
tend + to + Verb · opportunity to + Verb (or possibility of + -ing)

“Remote workers tend spend fewer hours commuting, which gives them the possibility to focus on deeper projects.”

👁️ Reveal Cambridge Model

“Remote workers tend to spend fewer hours commuting, which gives them the opportunity to focus on deeper projects.”

Keep to after tend, and use the opportunity to focus (or the possibility of focusing).

Check 5 · Agreement & Complete Clauses
Singular Head Noun + -s Verb · find that + Subject + Verb (or find + Noun)

“The constant noise of open-plan offices often reduce concentration, and many researchers find that their clearest ideas during quiet mornings at home.”

👁️ Reveal Cambridge Model

“The constant noise of open-plan offices often reduces concentration, and many researchers find that their clearest ideas emerge during quiet mornings at home.”

Singular head noun noise takes reduces, and find that needs a verb inside the clause (or drop that: find their clearest ideas).

Check 6 · Backwards Scan: Typos & “In the Case Of”
in the case of + Noun (= regarding) · Backwards pass catches typos & doubled words

“In case of densely populated coutnries, investing in the the public rail network eases traffic congestion.”

👁️ Reveal Cambridge Model

“In the case of densely populated countries, investing in the public rail network eases traffic congestion.”

In case of is for precautions (in case of fire), while in the case of introduces a situation; a 2-minute backwards read spots coutnries and the the instantly.
