Scenario 1

Scenario 2

“My mind went blank on an abstract Part 3 question!”

Buy 3 calm seconds by acknowledging the question, then split it into two simple sides.

👁️ Reveal Cambridge Model
“That is a good question; I haven’t thought about it in quite that way before. I suppose there are two sides to it: from a resident’s point of view…”
Scenario 3

“I finished all 4 bullets at 1 minute 20!”

Use the Compare & Future bridge: compare how you felt before vs after, or say whether you would return.

👁️ Reveal Cambridge Model
“Looking back on it now, what makes it stand out compared with newer places I’ve visited is… If I ever get the chance to go back, I’d definitely take…”
Scenario 4

“I forgot the exact English word mid-sentence!”

Paraphrase around it confidently. IELTS examiners actively reward smooth paraphrasing under Lexical Resource.

👁️ Reveal Cambridge Model
“They added… what’s the best way to put it, a curved open-air seating area for outdoor concerts, which completely lifted the atmosphere.”
