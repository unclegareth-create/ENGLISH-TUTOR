Four Habits to Fix

Habit 1 · Typing Slips Under the Clock

Ten words: constitued, argricultural, resonsibility, unattracrtive, conciously, continously, Swtizerland, conclustion, drawbacsk, extend (for extent). You know all of them. Keep the last two minutes for reading the essay from the final sentence back to the first.

Habit 2 · Openers With No Subject

While challenging, it is possible… The word challenging has nothing to describe. Name the subject: While this is challenging, …

Habit 3 · Agreement and Word Form

house recycling → household; households … is charged → are fined; how effective … are managed → how effectively. Ask of every verb: who is the subject, and is it plural?

Habit 4 · Task 1 Numbers

Check the dates against the chart, then recalculate each percentage: (new − old) ÷ old. For example, 42 → 40 is a fall of about 4.8%, and 5.18% of 42 takes you to 39.8, which is just under 40.
