Task 1 Rapid Accuracy Check · 4 Quick Fixes

Numbers, Labels & Past Tense Lock

Check every sentence against the rule before revealing the model.

Percentage Change = (New − Old) ÷ Old × 100  ·  Finished Past Years = Past Simple Only
Check 1 · Exact Percentage

Visitors to France rose from 75 million in 2000 to 90 million in 2020. Complete the sentence accurately using a percentage.

👁️ Reveal Cambridge Model
(90 − 75) ÷ 75 × 100 = 20%.
“Visitor numbers in France rose by 20%, from 75 million in 2000 to 90 million in 2020.”
Check 2 · Multiples

Tourists in Japan grew from 5 million in 2000 to 10 million in 2010 and 30 million in 2020. Describe this cleanly using doubled and six times.

👁️ Reveal Cambridge Model
“Arrivals in Japan doubled between 2000 and 2010 before surging to 30 million in 2020, six times as many as in 2000.”
Check 3 · Plural Subject & Past Tense

Fix the two errors: “In 2020, European destinations still attracts far more visitors then Japan.”

👁️ Reveal Cambridge Model
“In 2020, European destinations still attracted far more visitors than Japan.” (Past simple for 2020 + than for comparison.)
Check 4 · Opener Subject & Comparison

Fix the dangling opener: “Despite welcoming fewer tourists, the daily spending in Japan was 50% higher than in France.”

👁️ Reveal Cambridge Model
“Despite welcoming fewer tourists, Japan recorded average daily spending that was 50% higher than in France.” (Or: “Although Japan welcomed fewer tourists, its average daily spending was 50% higher than France’s.”)
