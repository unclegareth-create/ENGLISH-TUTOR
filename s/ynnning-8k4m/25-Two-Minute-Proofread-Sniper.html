Speaking Part 2 Bonus: The Past Simple Ignition Switch

In yesterday's practice test your Part 2 ideas and flow were strong—the only tiny wobble was opening a specific past story with the Present Perfect ("I have visited…"). Lock in Past Simple on Sentence 1 and the whole 2-minute turn flows effortlessly.

Specific Past Event Opening = Time Anchor (Last year / Two years ago / When I was at university) + Past Simple (visited / met / decided)

1. The 1-Minute Note Secret

Write only 4 single trigger keywords down the margin (one per bullet). Never write full sentences—your brain speaks faster from sparks than scripts.

2. The 3-Act Story Arc

Past Context (30s) → Sensory Snapshot / Core Scene (45s) → Reflection / 'So What?' (45s).

3. The Cambridge Anchor

Bullet points are springboards, not an interrogation checklist. If you finish early, deepen your reflection and comparison until the examiner stops you.

4 Quick-Fire Part 2 Opening Switches

Say the corrected opening out loud first, then reveal the model.

Cue Card 1 (A memorable hotel stay):
"I have stayed in a really impressive hotel in Zurich last summer."

Model: "Last summer, I stayed in a memorable boutique hotel in Zurich when I was attending a conference."
Why: Last summer locks the time in the finished past → Past Simple (stayed).

Cue Card 2 (A difficult decision you made):
"One of the biggest decisions I have made was when I have moved to Switzerland for my research."

Model: "One of the biggest decisions I've ever made was when I moved to Switzerland to join the research team at UNIL."
Why: Life-experience frame (ever made) can use Present Perfect, then immediately drop into Past Simple (moved) the moment you enter the specific event.

Cue Card 3 (A time someone gave you helpful advice):
"Two years ago, my supervisor has given me a piece of advice that changed how I work."

Model: "Two years ago, my supervisor gave me a piece of advice that completely changed how I approach my experiments."
Why: Any …ago marker always demands Past Simple (gave).

Cue Card 4 (An exhibition or cultural place you visited):
"I want to talk about a science museum that I have visited during a short trip to Geneva."

Model: "I'd like to talk about a science exhibition that I visited during a short trip to Geneva a few months ago."
Why: During a short trip names a finished occasion → Past Simple (visited).
