1. Past Simple Ignition

Specific past purchase or book = Past Simple from sentence one: "About six months ago, I ordered..." — never "I have bought..." for a finished event.

2. The 1-Minute Note Secret

Bullet points are springboards, not an interrogation checklist. If you finish early, deepen your reflection until halted.

Part 2 • Individual Long Turn (1m prep + 2m talk)

A Website You Have Bought Something From

Tense Trap Alert: Prompt uses Present Perfect → Switch to Past Simple for the purchase

Describe a website you have bought something from.

You should say:

  • what the website is
  • what you bought from this website
  • how satisfied you were with what you bought

and explain what you liked and disliked about using this website.

1-Minute Scratchpad (4 Keywords Only)
Clean Tense Transition (Present → Past Simple Lock)

“One website I use quite regularly is [Website], and about three months ago I ordered a [item] from there because my old one had finally given up. What drew me to it in the first place was the detailed customer reviews...”

Notice how anchoring a time marker (about three months ago) locks every following verb naturally into Past Simple.

Part 3 • Two-Way Discussion (4–5 mins)

Shopping Online & Online Retail Businesses

A.R.E.A.: Answer → Reason → Example → Alternative

Topic 1: Shopping online

1. What kinds of things do people in your country often buy from online shops?
2. Why has online shopping become so popular in many countries?
3. What are some possible disadvantages of buying things from online shops?

Topic 2: Online retail businesses

4. Do you agree that the prices of all goods should be lower on internet shopping sites than in shops?
5. Will large shopping malls continue to be popular, despite the growth of internet shopping?
6. Do you think that some businesses (e.g. banks and travel agents) will only operate online in the future?

Conceding & Balancing: "While online retailers clearly save on physical overheads, having said that, logistics and returns still cost a fortune."

Speculating on the Future: "I suspect high-street malls will evolve into social spaces rather than disappearing altogether."

Part 2 • Individual Long Turn (1m prep + 2m talk)

A Book That Made You Think a Lot

Pure Past Simple Narrative: was • decided • made • enjoyed

Describe a book that you enjoyed reading because you had to think a lot.

You should say:

  • what this book was
  • why you decided to read it
  • what reading this book made you think about

and explain why you enjoyed reading this book.

1-Minute Scratchpad (4 Keywords Only)
Clean Past Simple Opener

“A book that really stayed with me was [Title], which I picked up about two years ago after a colleague recommended it over lunch. On the surface it looked quite straightforward, but once I got into the second chapter, it forced me to rethink...”

Part 3 • Two-Way Discussion (4–5 mins)

Reading Habits & The Future of Books

A.R.E.A.: Answer → Reason → Example → Alternative

Topic 1: Reading & children / adults

1. What kinds of books are most popular with children in your country?
2. Why do some adults stop reading books for pleasure once they start working?
3. Do you think reading fiction is more valuable than reading non-fiction?

Topic 2: Digital reading & libraries

4. How has the rise of e-books and audiobooks changed the way people read?
5. Do you think public libraries will still be necessary in the future?
6. Should schools encourage students to read more classic literature or contemporary books?

Nuanced Comparison: "I wouldn't say one is inherently superior; fiction builds empathy, whereas non-fiction sharpens your analytical framework."

Cleft Emphasis: "What makes public libraries irreplaceable isn't just the books — it's having a quiet civic space that doesn't expect you to buy anything."

Part 2 • Individual Long Turn (1m prep + 2m talk)

Something You Bought for Your Home

Pure Past Simple Narrative: bought • chose • liked

Describe something you liked very much which you bought for your home.

You should say:

  • what you bought
  • when and where you bought it
  • why you chose this particular thing

and explain why you liked it so much.

1-Minute Scratchpad (4 Keywords Only)
Clean Past Simple Opener

“One of the best things I bought for my home was a [item], which I found in a small shop in [place] last winter. I had been looking for something compact and practical for weeks, and the moment I saw this one, I knew it would fit right in...”

Part 3 • Two-Way Discussion (4–5 mins)

Creating a Nice Home & Different Types of Home

A.R.E.A.: Answer → Reason → Example → Alternative

Topic 1: Creating a nice home

1. Why do some people spend a great deal of money decorating their homes?
2. What makes a home feel comfortable and welcoming to visitors?
3. Do you think the way people decorate their homes reflects their personality?

Topic 2: Housing & modern living

4. What are the main advantages and disadvantages of living in a flat compared with a house?
5. Why do many young people prefer to live in city centres rather than the suburbs?
6. How do you think the design of homes will change over the next twenty years?

Weighing Trade-Offs: "If I had to choose, I'd say convenience wins out in your twenties, whereas space and quiet start to matter far more later on."

Future Trends: "With remote work becoming standard, homes are increasingly designed as dual-purpose spaces rather than just places to sleep."
