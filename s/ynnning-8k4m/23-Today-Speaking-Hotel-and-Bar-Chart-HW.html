Part 2: Describe a Hotel That You Know

1 minute to jot 4 single trigger keywords, then 2 minutes using natural spoken signposting.

02:00
IELTS Speaking Part 2 Cue Card

Describe a hotel that you know.

You should say:

  • where this hotel is
  • what this hotel looks like
  • what facilities this hotel has
  • and explain whether you think this is a nice hotel to stay in.
Natural Spoken Signposting (Not "Firstly / Secondly")

1. Opening & Location: "The hotel I'd like to talk about is..." / "To set the scene, it's tucked away in..."

2. Moving to Appearance: "As for what it actually looks like..." / "In terms of its appearance..."

3. Moving to Facilities: "Moving on to the facilities it offers..." / "Turning to what's available on site..."

4. Moving to Your Verdict: "When it comes to whether it's a nice place to stay..." / "All things considered, I'd say..."

Quick Bridge Workout: Upgrade the Transition

1. "Secondly, what the hotel looks like: it is a glass building."

"As for what the hotel actually looks like, it's a modern glass building..."

2. "Thirdly, the facilities: it has a small gym and a rooftop bar."

"Moving on to discuss its facilities, there's a small gym as well as a rooftop bar..."

Part 3: Abstract Discussion (Hotels & Hospitality)

Use A.R.E.A. (Answer • Reason • Example • Alternative) and signpost comparisons smoothly.

Topic A: Staying in Hotels
  • What things are important when people are choosing a hotel?
  • Why do some people not like staying in hotels?
  • Do you think staying in a luxury hotel is a waste of money?
Topic B: Working in a Hotel
  • Do you think hotel work is a good career for life?
  • How does working in a big hotel compare with working in a small hotel?
  • What skills are needed to be a successful hotel manager?
