Mon 13:00 • IELTS Academic Task 1: The Overview & 6 Question Types
🎙️ Active Voice Warm-Up • 2-Minute Long Turn Challenge

Describe a natural place or park that you visited and enjoyed.

You should say:

  • where this natural place is and when you went there
  • what kind of plants, wildlife, or scenery you saw
  • what you did while you were there
  • and explain why protecting places like this is important for future generations.
1. The 1-Min Note Secret Write only 4 single trigger keywords down the margin. Never write full sentences.
2. The 3-Act Story Arc Past Context (30s) → Sensory Snapshot (45s) → Reflection / 'So What?' (45s).
3. Cambridge Anchor Bullet points are springboards, not an interrogation checklist. Deepen reflection!

10-Point IELTS Task 2 & C1 Structural Upgrade Table

Built directly from your Task 2 Essay Mark-Ups and Speaking Part 2/3 Diagnostics: turning Band 6.5/7.0 sentences into effortless Band 7.5-8.0 C1 structures.

1. Preparatory 'It' (Depersonalised)
It is widely argued that... / It is evident that...
2. Resultative Participle Clause
[Main Clause], thereby fostering / curbing + [Noun]
3. 'What' Cleft for Emphasis
What drives this trend is + [Noun / that-clause]
4. Negative Inversion
Not only does [Subject] [Verb], but it also [Verb]
