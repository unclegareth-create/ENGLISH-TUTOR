Fresh IELTS Task 2 Prompt (Two-Part Question)

In many cities today, fewer people know their immediate neighbours compared with previous generations.

Why is this happening, and what practical measures could strengthen community ties?

Write at least 250 words in 4 clear paragraphs (no mini-paragraphs).

P1 · Intro (~45w) Paraphrase the trend + preview both your main cause and your main measure.
P2 · Causes (~95w) 2 clear causes + 1 concrete example + 1 consequence sentence.
P3 · Measures (~95w) 2 realistic measures + 1 concrete example + 1 consequence sentence.
P4 · Closing (~40w) Fresh summary + your signature whether … depends on … closing.
C1 Structural Anchors:   What makes X difficult is…  |  Not only does X…, but it also…  |  …, thereby fostering…
