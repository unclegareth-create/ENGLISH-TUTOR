Separate Restrained C1 Model Essay

230 words · 4 paras (49 / 72 / 68 / 41w) · Inside 220–260 target

Music has been part of society since the dawn of humanity, serving as a vital means of communicating and expressing emotion. Modern culture highlights its value both in uniting social groups and in educating young children. It is widely acknowledged that music remains a key factor in human development.

First, music brings people together. Not only can listeners bond over a favourite singer or band, but they can also build lasting friendships through shared musical tastes. When Taylor Swift faced heavy criticism online, for instance, her fans rallied to defend someone they had never met. Even so, strong group loyalty has a downside. Rival fan bases sometimes turn hostile, occasionally ending in vandalism after concerts when cars or shopfronts are damaged.

Equally important is early childhood education. Young children absorb new ideas most naturally through patterns such as colours, shapes, and melody. It is the classic alphabet song that illustrates this best: children learn the tune long before reading the letters, which gives their early schooling a clear boost. Admittedly, music can also set a poor example if children hear violent themes or explicit lyrics unsuitable for their age.

In conclusion, while certain songs can be harmful, the benefits of music far outweigh its drawbacks. In my view, educating children is its more significant role, as early learning habits shape essential skills for life while still bringing people closer together.
