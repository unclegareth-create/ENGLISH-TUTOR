1. Speaking Debate: Systems, Privacy & Attention

0–20 mins

Pick two prompts below and defend a clear position using the A.R.E.A. frame.

Prompt A · Security vs Privacy

Kernel-Level Anti-Cheat

Should a game studio be allowed ring-0 access to your operating system simply to stop cheaters in multiplayer lobbies?

Prompt B · Economics of Scale

The Billion-Dollar Game

When a single release like GTA 6 costs over a billion dollars to build, does that scale stifle creative risk across the industry?

Prompt C · Virtual Markets

Paid Skins & Status

Why are rational players willing to spend real money on purely cosmetic items that carry zero gameplay advantage?

Prompt D · Cognitive Bandwidth

Deep Work vs Open Worlds

Can demanding open-world or competitive games coexist with intense academic study, or do they erode sustained focus?

A.R.E.A. Anchor: Answer directly → Reason (because / due to) → Example (concrete case) → Alternative (admittedly / having said that).
related with → related to
a knowledge about → knowledge of
waiting the release → waiting for the release
medias / informations → the media / information
wonder how does it work → wonder how it works
which creates hype → , thereby creating hype

2. Part 4 Constraint Puzzles

20–32 mins

Complete the second sentence using 3 to 6 words, including the key word. Never change the form of the key word.

4-Step Elimination: 1. Strike echoes → 2. Match tense → 3. Check voice → 4. Lock pattern (3–6 words)
Puzzle 01 INTENTION
Rockstar Games is not planning to release GTA 6 on PC at launch.
Rockstar Games has ________________________________ GTA 6 on PC at launch.
Model (4 words): no intention of releasing — Pattern: have no intention of + -ing.
Puzzle 02 RATHER
Many players think developers should fix server bugs instead of adding paid skins.
Many players ________________________________ server bugs instead of adding paid skins.
Model (4 words): would rather developers fixed — Pattern: would rather + subject + past simple (present/future preference).
Puzzle 03 TIME
Players must never share their account credentials with third-party boosting sites.
At ________________________________ share their account credentials with third-party boosting sites.
Model (4 words): no time must players — Pattern: negative inversion At no time + modal + subject + infinitive.
Puzzle 04 SPITE
Although kernel-level anti-cheat is controversial, most competitive players still install it.
Most competitive players still install kernel-level anti-cheat ________________________________ controversy.
Model (4 words): in spite of the — Pattern: in spite of + noun phrase (replacing although + clause).
Puzzle 05 SAID
People believe the studio spent over a billion dollars developing the game.
The studio ________________________________ over a billion dollars developing the game.
Model (5 words): is said to have spent — Pattern: passive reporting + perfect infinitive for a completed past action.

3. 8-Minute Micro-Writing Sprint

32–48 mins

Write one 80–90 word CAE body paragraph answering the prompt below. Include one C1 structure: What + Clause + BE, Not only + aux + subject, or It is widely argued that.

Prompt: Should governments regulate loot boxes and in-game microtransactions, or should game studios be trusted to self-regulate?
08:00
0 / 80–90 words
Cambridge Model (75 words):
It is widely argued that in-game microtransactions should be subject to strict statutory oversight rather than voluntary industry codes. What makes randomised loot boxes particularly problematic is their structural similarity to gambling, thereby exposing younger players to financial risk before they can fully evaluate odds. Admittedly, studios contend that recurring revenue funds ongoing server maintenance and free content updates. Nevertheless, transparent pricing and clear age-verification rules would protect consumers without undermining a studio’s commercial viability.
