Step 3 · Use of English Part 2: Open Cloze Sprint (1 Word Per Gap)

Read the short C1 paragraph on hosting major sporting events and type one grammatical word into each gap.

6 Gaps · Grammar Connectors
Hosting an international tournament is widely regarded (1) a major prestige project, yet (2) always do host cities recover the billions invested in stadiums. In (3) of the enormous public expense, supporters argue that such events inspire young people to take (4) regular physical exercise. (5) makes the debate so complex is that long-term health benefits are far harder to measure (6) immediate construction costs.
Cambridge Key: (1) as (regarded as) • (2) rarely / seldom / not (negative inversion: not always / rarely do host cities...) • (3) spite (in spite of) • (4) up (take up = start a sport/hobby) • (5) What (What-cleft: What makes... is...) • (6) than (harder to measure than).
