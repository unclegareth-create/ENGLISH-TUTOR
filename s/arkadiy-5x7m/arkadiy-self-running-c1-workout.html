Speaking Part 2 Cue Card • Prompt A

Describe a time when you had to concentrate on a complex task in a noisy or distracting environment.

You should say:

  • what the task was and why it mattered
  • what caused the noise or distractions around you
  • what practical steps you took to protect your focus
  • and explain what this experience taught you about how you work best.

Step 3 • Cambridge C1 Use of English Part 4

✂️ 8-Item Key Word Transformation Clinic

Complete the second sentence so that it has a similar meaning to the first sentence, using the word given. Do not change the word given. You must use between three and six words, including the word given.

1. Strike Out the Echoes Cross out repeated words from Sentence 1 to isolate the exact meaning gap.
2. Inspect Tense Harmony Match the timeframe of Sentence 1 (past simple, present perfect, modal).
3. Check Voice Shift Notice if the subject shifts from active to passive or personal to impersonal.
4. Solve the Collocation Fit the deficit into a 3–6 word C1 phrase without altering the keyword.
