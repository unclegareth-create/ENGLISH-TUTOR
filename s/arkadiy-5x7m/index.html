🎙️ Active Voice Warm-Up • 2-Minute Long Turn Challenge

Describe a difficult problem you solved at work or in your studies.

You should say:

  • what the problem was
  • when it happened
  • what you did to solve it
  • and explain what you learned from this experience.
1. The 1-Min Note Secret Write only 4 single trigger keywords down the margin. Never write full sentences.
2. The 3-Act Story Arc Past Context (30s) → Sensory Snapshot (45s) → Reflection / 'So What?' (45s).
3. Cambridge Anchor Bullet points are springboards, not an interrogation checklist. Deepen reflection!
