🎙️ Active Voice Warm-Up • 2-Minute Long Turn Challenge

Describe a piece of music or musical performance that deeply moved or inspired you

  • What the piece of music, performance, or composer was and when you first encountered it
  • What specific acoustic, melodic, or production qualities caught your attention
  • Why you turn to this music and how your perception of it has changed over time
  • And reflect on how music influences emotional focus and cognitive flow under pressure
1. The 1-Min Note Secret Write only 4 single trigger keywords down the margin. Never write full sentences.
2. The 3-Act Story Arc Past Context (30s) → Sensory Snapshot (45s) → Reflection / 'So What?' (45s).
3. Cambridge Anchor Bullet points are springboards, not an interrogation checklist. Deepen reflection!

💡 C1 Structural Formula Workout

Interactive Reveal • Cambridge Model
Formula 01: Cleft Sentences to Replace Hesitation Fillers Under Pressure
What + [Clause / Musical Feature] + BE + [Emphasised Element / Noun Phrase]

Drill Prompt: Replace filler words ('like, you know, sort of') when stressed: 'I really love acoustic music because it feels authentic and grounded.'

→ "What draws me so strongly to acoustic arrangements is the organic authenticity that synthetic production rarely replicates."
