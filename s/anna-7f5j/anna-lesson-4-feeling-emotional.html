Exercises 18 & 19 · Simon’s Bad Day & Final Discussion

Read the short case study and discuss how Simon could have handled the situation differently.

Simon had a very stressful day at work. First, his train was delayed, so he was late. Then he spilled coffee on his shirt before a meeting with an important client. Finally, his boss was very critical of his work and demanded that he stay late to rewrite his report. On the train home, somebody stepped on Simon’s foot, his emotions got the better of him and he shouted at them.
  • Ex 18: How could Simon have handled his emotions differently? What would you have done in his situation?
  • Ex 19.1: How would the world be different if everyone expressed exactly how they were feeling at all times?
  • Ex 19.2: Would the world be better or worse?
