Warm-up · Speaking Part 3 · Two minutes, your voice first

Why might studying for an exam be beneficial?

Think about:

  • what you learn along the way
  • what a result or certificate gives you afterwards
  • whether studying for a test can be more motivating than studying without one

A · Answer

Give your view in one clear sentence: “I think it can be very beneficial, mainly because…”

R · Reason

Say why. One strong reason is better than three vague ones.

E · Example

Make it real: a time from your own life, or someone you know.

A · Alternative

Finished early? Look at the other side: “On the other hand, some people argue that…” Then say which view you find more convincing.

Part 1 · Multiple-choice cloze

Which word is the natural partner?

The article is called Adventure Travel. All four options mean roughly the same thing, but only one belongs in the phrase.

Fixed phrase = Word A + Word B (beaten + ____)
3…exploring not only some exotic of the globe, but also…
A corner   B edge   C angle   D pocket
A · corner: “a corner of the world” is the set phrase.
4…anyone willing to forsake the beaten …
A path   B road   C track   D course
C · track: “off the beaten track” is the only fixed phrase.

Part 5 · Multiple choice · A novel extract

Read for the idea, not the word.

Anxiously, she scoured the press for hints of impending issues. In this trade, she saw, you needed not so much to be abreast of things as ahead of them, lying in wait for circumstance, ready to pounce.
32Lucy thinks the secret of success as a freelance journalist is to
A keep yourself informed about current affairs.
B adopt a controversial style of writing.
C identify future newsworthy situations.
D make as many contacts as possible.
C · “Ahead of” things means spotting what is coming. A is the trap: “abreast of things” is exactly what the text says is not enough.
Part 6 · Gapped text · The fog forest

A bare island where rain never falls could soon be covered with trees.

Seven paragraphs have been removed from the article. Your job is to put them back. Clues hide in the first and last lines.

Paragraph H

The bare hills in this region have been… Despite numerous attempts in the past decade, all replanting schemes have so far been unsuccessful. With limited water supplies on the island, the newly planted trees dried out and died.

The gap is followed by

“For years Riebold watched these failed efforts by local foresters. Then he read about a successful research project in Chile…”

The link: “these failed efforts” points straight back to “unsuccessful replanting schemes”. Reference words (this, these, such, that) and repeated ideas are your best clues. There is one extra paragraph you will not use.
