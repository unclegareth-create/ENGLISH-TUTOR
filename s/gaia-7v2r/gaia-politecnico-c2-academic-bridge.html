Step 2 • C2 Quantitative & Technical Prose Blueprints

4 High-Precision C2 Academic Structures & False-Friend Detox

Practise the four structures below—designed for quantitative research reports, seminar papers, and eliminating Italian-to-English interference—then click 👁️ Reveal Cambridge Model.

Blueprint 1 • Contrastive It-Clefts Distinguishing Primary Causes
It is not so much + [Noun Phrase X] + as + [Noun Phrase Y] + that + [Verb Phrase]

Upgrade This Sentence: "Raw computing speed does not guarantee an accurate simulation; rather, well-chosen boundary conditions guarantee it."

C2 Academic Model: It is not so much raw computing speed as well-chosen boundary conditions that guarantee an accurate simulation.
Blueprint 2 • Resultative Participle Chains Replacing ', which causes...'
[Main Technical Clause], thereby + [Present Participle -ing] + [Quantifiable Outcome]

Upgrade This Sentence: "Regularisation penalises overly complex parameters, and this prevents the statistical model from overfitting noisy data."

C2 Academic Model: Regularisation penalises overly complex parameters, thereby preventing the statistical model from overfitting noisy data.
Blueprint 3 • Passive Reporting with Prior Action Literature Review Register
Subject + is / are + [widely / generally] + thought / believed / known + to have + Past Participle (V3)

Upgrade This Sentence: "Historians of mathematics believe that early probability theory originated in seventeenth-century correspondence."

C2 Academic Model: Early probability theory is widely thought to have originated in seventeenth-century correspondence.
Blueprint 4 • Italian-to-English C2 False-Friend Detox Precision Register
instead (invece) → by contrast / rather | exaggerated (esagerato) → excessive | deceivable → deceptive | implements → facilities

Repair All 3 False Friends: "Linear models converge rapidly; instead, non-linear approximations require an exaggerated amount of time and can produce deceivable graphs."

C2 Academic Model: Linear models converge rapidly; by contrast, non-linear approximations require an excessive amount of time and can produce deceptive (or: misleading) graphs.
