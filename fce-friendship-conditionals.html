3. Conditionals Beyond “If” & 4-Step Part 4 Workout

6 Items • 12 Marks (1 + 1 Split)

Cambridge examiners test whether you can handle mixed conditionals, unreal past wishes, and high-level alternatives to if:

Had it not been for / But for

Formula: Had it not been for + Noun, Subject + would have + V3
“But for your advice, I would have given up.”

Wish / If only + Past Perfect

Formula: Subject + wish / If only + Subject + had (not) + V3
“If only I had stayed in touch with him!”

Unless / Provided (that) / Supposing

Formula: Unless + Affirmative Verb | Provided that / Supposing + Clause
“Supposing he refuses to apologise, what will you do?”

Would rather + Past Subjunctive

Formula: Subject 1 + would rather + Subject 2 + Past Simple
“I’d rather you didn’t mention this to anyone.”
