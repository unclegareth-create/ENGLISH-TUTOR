Why IRATA Level 3 Matters

Not Just a Climber.
A Fully Accountable Safety Lead.

In the UK and Europe, working on ropes is governed by strict statutory instruments (Work at Height Regulations 2005, BS 7985, and IRATA ICOP). Hiring a contractor without an active, certified Level 3 on-site exposes facility owners and main contractors to serious legal liability.

Common Questions

Industrial Rope Access FAQ

Key questions answered for facilities managers, structural surveyors, and principal contractors.

Why choose rope access over scaffolding or cherry pickers (MEWPs)?
Rope access delivers 60% to 80% cost savings compared to scaffolding on high-rise and awkward structures. Setup takes hours instead of weeks, no expensive municipal street or pavement permits are required, and equipment is completely de-rigged at the end of each working day—eliminating the severe security risk of intruders climbing scaffolds after hours.
What is the role of an IRATA Level 3 Safety Supervisor?
An IRATA Level 3 is the highest qualification in industrial rope access, requiring extensive logged field experience, rigorous rescue and rigging examinations, and comprehensive knowledge of statutory safety legislation. The Level 3 is responsible for drafting and signing off the site-specific Risk Assessment & Method Statement (RAMS), inspecting all rigging gear, and maintaining pre-rigged rescue capability.
What if our building does not have certified roof eyebolts or anchor points?
That is standard practice. We utilize mobile deadweight anchor trolleys, parapet clamps, structural steel beam strops, and certified rigging tripods that require zero structural drilling or roof penetrations. If required, we can also test and certify permanent anchor bolts to BS EN 795 standards.
How quickly can you respond to an emergency situation?
For high-risk emergency situations—such as storm-damaged cladding, loose masonry over pedestrian walkways, or fractured overhead glass—we maintain rapid mobilization capability across Northern Ireland, Ireland, and mainland UK.
Do you provide full insurance and photographic condition reports?
Yes. After every inspection or repair project, you receive an engineer-ready digital PDF report with high-resolution defect imagery, location details, and remedial recommendations.
