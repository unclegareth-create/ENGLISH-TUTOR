The Premise: High Stakes, High Diplomacy across 4 Acts

In international business, having the best technical analysis is only half the battle. If you tell a Chief Financial Officer, “We don't have enough money for this phase”, or tell a Commercial Director, “Your proposal makes no sense”, you may be factually right, yet you immediately erode your authority in the room. Senior executive communication relies on C1 syntactic softening: depersonalising objections, projecting timelines objectively, and framing refusals as strategic trade-offs.

The Boardroom Pitch now spans 20 high-stakes scenarios grouped into 4 Acts, complete with a live Scenario Selector, an Untimed Roleplay Mode for 1-to-1 coaching or self-study, and an interactive 👁️ Reveal C1 Diplomatic Blueprint panel on every turn:

Core C1 Diplomatic Blueprints You Will Master

  • Preparatory ‘It’ for Impersonal Caution:
    It remains doubtful whether + Subject + Modal + Verb...
    “It remains doubtful whether we can deliver this scope without a modest budget reallocation.”
  • Passive Reporting for Objective Timelines:
    Subject + BE + projected/estimated + to-infinitive
    “The timeline is projected to require an additional two weeks to pass full security auditing.”
  • Softened Negative Inversion for Quality Standards:
    Rarely + Auxiliary (do/does/did) + Subject + Bare Infinitive
    “Rarely do we see rushed deployments succeed without a 48-hour quality assurance buffer.”
  • Inverted Conditionals for Strategic Trade-Offs:
    Should + Subject + Bare Infinitive... / Were + Subject + to-infinitive...
    “Should we reallocate engineering capacity to these new features now, we would need to revise the Q3 budget.”
Cambridge C1 Use of English Part 4

10-Item Executive Diplomatic Transformation Workout

Complete the second sentence so that it has a similar, more diplomatic meaning to the first sentence, using the word given. Do not change the word given. You must use between three and six words, including the word given.

Score: 0 / 10
1. Strike Out Echoes Cross out repeated words in Sentence 1 to isolate the exact semantic deficit.
2. Tense Harmony Match the present, past, or conditional timeframe of the original thought.
3. Voice & Frame Shift Check whether personal active shifts to impersonal It, passive, or inversion.
4. 3–6 Word Lock Keep the keyword 100% unchanged and count 3 to 6 words total.
