Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy explains how Gareth Montgomery English Tutoring collects, uses, and protects any information that you provide when using this website.

Information Collection

When you use the contact form to enquire or book a trial lesson, or when you request a study guide, the following information may be collected:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Your current English level, target exam, and learning goals

Use of Information

The information collected is used exclusively for:

  • Responding to your enquiries and scheduling lessons
  • Delivering requested study guides and cheat sheets
  • Tailoring lesson materials and feedback to your goals
  • Invoicing and administrative records

We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your personal information with any commercial third parties.

Web Analytics & Cookies

This website uses GoatCounter for privacy-friendly web analytics. GoatCounter is completely cookieless, does not set tracking cookies on your device, does not track visitors across websites, and does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) or IP addresses. It complies fully with UK GDPR, EU GDPR, and PECR without requiring an intrusive cookie consent banner. Aggregate metrics (such as page views and general referring sources) are stored solely to understand website usage and improve learning resources.

Local Browser Storage (localStorage) & Interactive Engines

Our interactive learning modules (including the Candidate Dossier & Mistake Vault, Live Essay Syntax Stress-Tester, Dark Academia Course Gate, and Teacher Presenter HUD) store your practice scores, grammar drill history, draft text, and licence unlock status locally in your browser’s localStorage. This data remains strictly on your own device and is never automatically transmitted to any external database. It is only shared with me if you voluntarily copy or include your diagnostic dossier code when submitting an enquiry or booking a session.

Speaking Practice & Microphone Permissions

When you click to practise spoken sentences in the Speaking Practice tool on the Interactive Lab page, your browser may request temporary microphone access via the Web Audio API and Web Speech API. To turn your speech into text, browsers that support the Web Speech API (such as Google Chrome) may send the audio to their speech recognition provider (for example, Google’s servers). Mozilla Firefox does not support browser speech recognition, and you can switch to the tap-rhythm and typing mode at any time without turning on your microphone. Gareth Montgomery English never records, stores, or saves your voice audio on its own servers.

Live P2P Classroom Sync & Licensed Third-Party Tutors

When a student explicitly opens a live classroom join link (?room=...) shared by their tutor, the browser establishes an ephemeral peer-to-peer connection using BroadcastChannel (for local tabs) or PeerJS WebRTC DataChannels (which uses standard WebRTC STUN/signalling to connect the student and tutor browsers directly). During an active room session, only text typed into lesson exercise inputs is streamed to the hosting tutor’s screen; contact forms, email fields, and password fields are strictly excluded, and no keystrokes are logged or stored on any server. Independent tutors and language schools using licensed White-Label Homework Links (?wl=1) act as independent Data Controllers responsible for their own student relationships and compliance with applicable local privacy and minor-protection laws.

Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services that may process data when you interact with them:

  • FormSubmit: Used to securely route contact form enquiries, waitlist registrations, and cheat sheet requests to my inbox. An automated one-time response containing download links for requested study materials is sent via FormSubmit. If you request the free study pack, I only use your email address to send the pack unless you explicitly ask for lessons or further information. You can request complete data deletion at any time by replying to any email.
  • Cal.com & Stripe: Used for 1-to-1 trial lesson scheduling and secure payment processing. Financial data is handled directly by Stripe in compliance with PCI-DSS standards.
  • Vimeo: Embedded video clips are configured with “Do Not Track” (DNT) enabled to minimise cookie usage and protect your privacy.

Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to request access to the personal data held about you, request correction of any inaccurate details, or request complete erasure of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact me directly via the website contact form.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy or would like your details removed, please reach out directly via the contact form on the homepage.
