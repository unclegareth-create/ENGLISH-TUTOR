Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy explains how Gareth Montgomery English Tutoring collects, uses, and protects any information that you give when you use this website.

Information Collection

When you use the contact form to book a trial lesson or inquire about services, the following information is collected:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Your current English level and learning goals

Use of Information

The information collected is used exclusively for:

  • Responding to your inquiries and scheduling lessons
  • Understanding your needs to provide tailored tutoring services
  • Invoicing and administrative purposes

We do not share, sell, distribute, or lease your personal information to third parties unless we have your explicit permission or are required by law to do so.

Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services that may process data:

  • FormSubmit: Used to securely route your contact form submissions to my email.
  • Cal.com & Stripe: Used for scheduling lessons and processing payments securely.
  • Vimeo: Embedded videos are configured in "Do Not Track" mode to minimize cookie usage, but Vimeo may still collect anonymous usage data.
  • Google AdSense: Third-party vendor Google uses cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy or would like your data deleted, please reach out directly via the contact form on the homepage.
