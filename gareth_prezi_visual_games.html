✂️ Gareth's 4-Step Elimination & Deconstruction Strategy

Before writing a word into the gap, strip away the repeated language from Sentence 1 so you can see the exact grammar transformation left behind.

1. Strike Out the Echoes
Cross out words from Sentence 1 already repeated in Sentence 2 to isolate the exact meaning gap.
2. Inspect Tense Harmony
Match Sentence 1's time frame and aspect (past, perfect, or hypothetical) to the new stem.
3. Check Voice Shift
Spot active-to-passive shifts or fronted inversions triggered by the opening words.
4. Solve the Pattern
Fit 3 to 6 words using the keyword unchanged (contractions = 2 words, cannot = 1).
