🏝️ 1. The Story

Villa Bourani, Greece (1953)

You arrive on the Aegean island of Phraxos as the new English teacher. At the cliffside estate of Bourani, the enigmatic millionaire Maurice Conchis has staged a psychological illusion—his “Godgame.”

🪨 2. How You Play

4 Altars • 3 Stone Columns

Before each of the 4 Marble Altars sits a rack of 15 scrambled stone tablets in 3 columns (Beginning • Middle • Ending). Click 1 stone from each column to build a complete 3-part sentence.

⚡ 3. What Happens

Shatter 20 Island Illusions

Every valid sentence (5 per Altar, 20 total) fires a skyward beam of light and physically transforms the 3D island—raising sunken staircases, summoning Greek ships, and awakening celestial machines!
