Museum Words (Passive Decoding) Nautical Victorian
Historical maritime vocabulary. Vital for reading comprehension, but inappropriate for modern corporate or academic prose.
Schooner [noun] A sailing ship with two or more masts. Use vessel or cargo ship in modern reports.
Bight [noun] A curve or recess in a coastline; a loop in a rope. Strictly geographical or nautical.
Coxswain [noun] The steersman of a ship's boat or racing crew.
Arsenal Words (Active C1/C2 Lexis) High-Yield Production
Elite formal vocabulary essential for forensic case studies, academic reviews, and Cambridge C2 writing.
Unaccountably [adverb] In a way that cannot be explained or accounted for; mysteriously. Collocates: unaccountably delayed, vanish unaccountably.
Derelict [adjective / noun] In a very poor condition as a result of disuse and neglect; abandoned. Collocates: derelict vessel, derelict building.
Disclose [verb] To make secret or new information known; allow something hidden to be seen. Collocates: disclose confidential data, disclose findings.

2. Syntactic Architecture: Impersonal Passives & Nominalisation

C2 Forensic & Investigative Register

In official journalism, legal reports, and academic research, impersonal passives and nominalisation allow writers to present unconfirmed hypotheses or objective findings with authoritative detachment:

1. Impersonal 'It'-Passive

Distances the reporter from unverified claims:

• It is alleged that an unidentified creature leapt from the prow.
(= People allege that...)

2. Subject-Raised Passive Infinitives

Focuses directly on the entity being investigated:

• The crew are understood to have perished prior to landfall.
(= Authorities understand that the crew perished...)

3. Forensic Nominalisation

Packs verbal events into conceptual noun phrases:

• The dereliction of duty and subsequent evacuation remain unverified.

4. The Perfect Passive Infinitive Trap

Ensure completed past events take to have been + past participle:

• ✗ The captain is thought to be murdered days earlier.
• ✓ The captain is thought to have been murdered days earlier.
