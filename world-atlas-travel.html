Quick Boarding-Pass Reference: The 8 Target Adjectives

✔ Positive Places (4)

  • Picturesque: attractive enough for a postcard or photograph.
  • Vibrant: full of energy, colour, music, and life.
  • Tranquil: calm, peaceful, with very little noise.
  • Cosmopolitan: international, shaped by many cultures.

✘ Negative Places (4)

  • Overrated: praised by everyone, but disappointing in reality.
  • Overcrowded: packed with far too many people.
  • Touristy: built for visitors rather than locals, artificial.
  • Run down: neglected, damaged, and in poor condition.
