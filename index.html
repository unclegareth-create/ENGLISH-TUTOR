In many modern cities, traffic congestion is a growing problem. Cars cause a lot of pollution, and they also waste people's time every day.Not only do private vehicles exacerbate urban air pollution, but they also erode daily productivity.1 Therefore, governments must take decisive action. About Me

Career Educator. Cambridge Specialist. 16+ Years at the Whiteboard & the Screen.

Hi, I’m Gareth. I didn’t stumble into online tutoring as a side gig—teaching English has been my career since qualifying with my Cambridge CELTA at Belfast Metropolitan College in 2008.

I started teaching immediately in an international summer school held in the historic main building of Queen’s University Belfast, followed by a year teaching in Japan at an Aikawa-shien. Returning home, I was recruited back by my former CELTA tutor to join the faculty at Belfast Metropolitan College, where I taught for the next 15 years.

In the college classroom, I taught across the entire spectrum—from entry-level English for newly arrived refugees building a life from scratch, to international professionals and exam candidates. For over eight of those years, my core specialty was intensive Cambridge Exam preparation (FCE, CAE, and CPE)—building proprietary frameworks, interactive slide decks, and targeted materials to crack the exam board mark schemes.

When teaching moved online during COVID, I brought that formal classroom rigor with me—first coaching international executives and young learners across Asia with iTutor, and now running my own independent tuition practice. Every lesson is one-to-one, structured, and focused: whether you are preparing for IELTS/Cambridge or speaking in high-stakes professional meetings, we skip the filler and work directly on what actually moves your score.

Tested experience: 15 years college classroom lecturer, over 2,000 hours of online teaching across 2,100+ individual lessons, and a 4.9/5 average student rating.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about lesson delivery, time zones, levels, and booking.

What platform do we use, and which time zones do you teach?
All private 1-to-1 sessions take place online via Zoom or Google Meet with HD audio and live screen sharing. Based in Northern Ireland (UK time), I teach weekday and weekend slots that suit students across Europe, East Asia, the Middle East, and the Americas.
How does a typical lesson run?
Each 50-minute session is relaxed, interactive, and focused on getting you speaking. Depending on your goals, we might work on natural conversation and vocabulary, unpack specific Cambridge or IELTS tasks, or rehearse upcoming presentations. We finish with clear feedback, pronunciation tips, and a shared Google Doc of notes.
Which English levels do you accept?
I specialise in intermediate-to-advanced learners (CEFR B1+ up to C2 Proficiency): exam candidates targeting Cambridge or IELTS, professionals using English at work, and lifelong or heritage learners who want to speak more naturally.

Who I'm not right for: I do not accept complete beginners (A1/A2).
Do you provide lesson materials and writing feedback?
Yes, all lesson materials—custom slides, authentic Cambridge/IELTS sample tasks and past-paper practice materials, interactive presentations, and writing prompts—are provided free of charge. Every student gets a shared Google Doc for class notes, and students on 5-, 10-, or 12-lesson packages (or our standalone £39 Async Audit) also receive between-class markups on essays or emails.
What is your cancellation and package refund policy?
Life happens! Any lesson can be rescheduled or cancelled with at least 12 hours' advance notice at no cost. Multi-lesson packages are valid for 6 months from purchase, and if your circumstances change, any unused sessions in a pack can be refunded pro-rata.
How does payment work?
When booking your £20 diagnostic lesson on the calendar below, payment is handled securely at checkout via Stripe. For 5-, 10-, or 12-lesson packages, single sessions, or 24-hour Async Audits, you can pay securely via Stripe, PayPal, or Wise.
