Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about lesson delivery, time zones, levels, and booking.

What platform do we use, and which time zones do you teach?
All private 1-on-1 sessions are delivered online via Zoom or Google Meet with HD audio and live screen sharing. Based in Northern Ireland (UK time), I offer weekday and weekend slots accommodating students across Europe, East Asia, the Middle East, and the Americas.
How does a typical lesson run?
Each 50-minute session is highly interactive and maximizes your speaking time. We start with targeted active recall or error correction, transition into high-intensity exam practice or speech delivery (e.g. Cambridge Speaking Part 2/3 or IELTS Task 2 analysis), and finish with immediate diagnostic feedback, pronunciation polish, and agreed homework tasks.
Which English levels do you accept?
My specialty is intermediate-to-advanced learners: CEFR B1+ up to C2 Proficiency, as well as IELTS candidates targeting Band 7.0+.

Who I'm not right for: I do not currently accept complete beginners (A1/A2). This ensures every session can remain focused on advancing your syntax, exam strategy, and fluency.
Do you provide lesson materials and homework?
Yes, completely free of charge. You get custom lecture slides, authentic Cambridge exam papers, proprietary interactive presentations (Prezis), and structured writing prompts. I also provide line-by-line markups and feedback on writing submissions between lessons.
What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?
Life happens! Lessons can be rescheduled or cancelled with at least 12 hours advance notice at zero fee. We simply find another slot in the calendar that fits your schedule.
How does payment work?
Payments are processed securely via Stripe, PayPal, or Wise. You can pay for single lessons on a pay-as-you-go basis, or purchase a discounted multi-lesson package upfront. An invoice link will be sent to you prior to the lesson.
