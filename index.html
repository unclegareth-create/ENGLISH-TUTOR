Prompt

"Discuss two ways governments can encourage people to use public transport instead of private cars, and evaluate which is more effective."

Communicative Achievement: 3/5 → 5/5
Lexical Resource: B2 → C2
Grammatical Range: 3/5 → 5/5
Gareth's Line-by-Line Markup CEFR B2 → C1/C2
Click any numbered highlight [1–4] to inspect why

In many modern cities, traffic congestion is a growing problem. Cars cause a lot of pollution, and they also waste people's time every day.Not only do private vehicles exacerbate urban air pollution, but they also erode daily productivity.1 Therefore, governments must take decisive action. First, if the government makes train and bus tickets cheaper,First and foremost, heavily subsidising rail and bus fares would allow authorities to2 encourage low-income commuters to leave their cars at home. However, cheap tickets are not enough if the buses are always late.Nevertheless, affordability alone is futile if the existing network remains unreliable.3 Because of this, I think spending money on faster trains is the best choice.Consequently, channelling investment into high-frequency infrastructure is by far the more viable long-term strategy.4

B2 / Repetitive Phrasing C1/C2 Upgrade 1 Examiner Criterion Note
GE
Gareth's Diagnostic Verdict 15+ Yrs Lecturer Experience

Your argument is logical, which gets you a pass at B2. However, to hit a Grade A (C2 level) in Cambridge Advanced, we need to replace conversational verbs like 'make tickets cheaper' with C1 noun phrases ('subsidising fares') and showcase at least one complex emphatic structure like Negative Inversion.

Line-by-Line Examiner Notes

Why each change boosts your official score

Inspecting Note #1 of 4
1 Negative Inversion (Grammar)
Grammatical Range & Accuracy
✗ "Cars cause a lot of pollution, and they also waste people's time every day."
✓ "Not only do private vehicles exacerbate urban air pollution, but they also erode daily productivity."

Why it scores higher: Cambridge examiners actively look for C1/C2 grammar structures. Starting with 'Not only do...' (Negative Inversion) immediately signals advanced control, while upgrading 'cause a lot of' to 'exacerbate' boosts lexical precision.

2 Nominalisation & Collocation
Lexical Resource
✗ "First, if the government makes train and bus tickets cheaper,"
✓ "First and foremost, heavily subsidising rail and bus fares would allow authorities to"

Why it scores higher: Replacing the basic conditional ('if the government makes... cheaper') with a gerund phrase and C1 collocation ('heavily subsidising... fares') creates an authoritative, academic register.

3 C1 Cohesive Device
Organisation & Cohesion
✗ "However, cheap tickets are not enough if the buses are always late."
✓ "Nevertheless, affordability alone is futile if the existing network remains unreliable."

Why it scores higher: Avoid repeating concrete nouns ('tickets', 'buses'). Using abstract nouns ('affordability alone', 'existing network') and 'Nevertheless' elevates your Organisation and Communicative Achievement marks.

4 Evaluative Conclusion
Content & Register
✗ "Because of this, I think spending money on faster trains is the best choice."
✓ "Consequently, channelling investment into high-frequency infrastructure is by far the more viable long-term strategy."

Why it scores higher: Part 1 Essays require you to evaluate which option is stronger. 'I think... is the best choice' sounds B1/B2. 'By far the more viable long-term strategy' nails the persuasive C1 academic tone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about lesson delivery, time zones, levels, and booking.

What platform do we use, and which time zones do you teach?
All private 1-on-1 sessions are delivered online via Zoom or Google Meet with HD audio and live screen sharing. Based in Northern Ireland (UK time), I offer weekday and weekend slots accommodating students across Europe, East Asia, the Middle East, and the Americas.
How does a typical lesson run?
Each 50-minute session is relaxed, highly interactive, and maximises your speaking time. Depending on your goals, we might focus on natural conversation and vocabulary, dive into specific exam tasks (like Cambridge or IELTS speaking/writing), or polish professional presentations. We finish with clear, encouraging feedback, pronunciation tips, and agreed practice for next time.
Which English levels do you accept?
I specialise in intermediate-to-advanced learners: CEFR B1+ up to C2 Proficiency, exam candidates targeting Cambridge or IELTS 7.0+, and professionals or adults looking to speak with greater fluency.

Who I'm not right for: I do not currently accept complete beginners (A1/A2). This ensures every session stays focused on building your confidence, vocabulary, and natural fluency.
Do you provide lesson materials and homework?
Yes, completely free of charge. You get custom lecture slides, authentic Cambridge exam papers, proprietary interactive presentations (Prezis), and structured writing prompts. I also provide line-by-line markups and feedback on writing submissions between lessons.
What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?
Life happens! Lessons can be rescheduled or cancelled with at least 12 hours advance notice at zero fee. We simply find another slot in the calendar that fits your schedule.
How does payment work?
Payments are processed securely via Stripe, PayPal, or Wise. You can pay for single lessons on a pay-as-you-go basis, or purchase a discounted multi-lesson package upfront. An invoice link will be sent to you prior to the lesson.
