How I mark your writing

How a B2 draft becomes C1 / Band 7.5+ writing

Good feedback explains why one phrasing works better than another. Here’s the kind of change we make together.

Before and after: how I mark writing
The pattern: Rather than + V-ing ..., Subject + ought to + Verb, thereby + V-ing ...
Student draft Everyday Phrasing
“Universities should focus more on practical skills instead of just theory, because this will help graduates find jobs much faster after they leave.”
Relies on repetitive causal links (‘because this will help’) and flat clause rhythm.
How we’d improve it C1 / Band 8.0 Standard
“Rather than prioritising abstract theory, universities ought to anchor their curricula in practical training, thereby equipping graduates to navigate an increasingly competitive job market.”

Hi, I’m Gareth. 19 Years Teaching, 15 of Them at Belfast Met.

I qualified with my Cambridge CELTA at Belfast Metropolitan College in 2007 and have spent the 19 years since helping students feel genuinely comfortable, accurate, and confident in English.

After starting out at an international summer school at Queen’s University Belfast and spending a year teaching in Japan, I returned home to join the faculty at Belfast Metropolitan College, where I taught for 15 years.

At Belfast Met, I taught everyone from newly arrived refugees building a life from scratch to international professionals and exam candidates. For over eight of those years, I specialised in Cambridge exam preparation (B2 First, C1 Advanced, and C2 Proficiency), helping hundreds of college and private students pass Cambridge exams and reach the IELTS score they needed by finding clear, practical ways to explain tricky grammar and essay structure so lessons never feel dry or intimidating.

Since moving online in 2020, I’ve delivered more than 2,100 one-to-one lessons. My students often tell me our sessions are a relaxed highlight of their week: I always bring a clear plan to keep us focused on your goal, while giving you the space to speak naturally and express what you actually mean.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about lesson delivery, time zones, levels, and booking.

What platform do we use, and which time zones do you teach?
All private 1-to-1 sessions take place online via Zoom or Google Meet with HD audio and live screen sharing. Based in Northern Ireland (UK time), I teach weekday and weekend slots that suit students across Europe, East Asia, the Middle East, and the Americas.
How does a typical lesson run?
Each 50-minute session is relaxed, interactive, and focused on getting you speaking. Depending on your goals, we might work on natural conversation and vocabulary, unpack specific Cambridge or IELTS tasks, or rehearse upcoming presentations. We finish with clear feedback, pronunciation tips, and a shared Google Doc of notes.
Which English levels do you accept?
My 1-to-1 coaching is designed for intermediate-to-advanced adult learners (CEFR B1+ up to C2 Proficiency): exam candidates targeting Cambridge or IELTS, professionals using English at work, and lifelong or heritage learners who want to speak more naturally.

Teens & Young Learners: Inside the Interactive Lab, teens (B1–B2 PET/FCE) can play Chronicle ’88 (an 80s-style detective adventure game), while younger learners (A1–B1) can explore Professor West’s 3D Study.
Do you provide lesson materials and writing feedback?
Yes, all lesson materials (custom interactive slides, official Cambridge English sample papers, official IELTS sample test questions, and writing prompts) are provided free of charge. Every student gets a shared Google Doc for class notes, and students on 5-, 10-, or 12-lesson packs (or £29 written essay feedback) also receive between-class comments on essays or emails.
What is your cancellation and package refund policy?
Life happens! Any lesson can be rescheduled or cancelled with at least 12 hours' advance notice at no cost. Multi-lesson packs are valid for 6 months from your first lesson, and any unused sessions in a pack are refunded pro-rata at the per-lesson rate of your pack (£75/lesson on the 5-lesson pack; £65/lesson on the 10- and 12-lesson packs, less any £35 starter session credit applied).
How does payment work?
When booking your £35 30-minute starter session on the calendar below, payment is handled securely at checkout via Stripe. For 5-, 10-, or 12-lesson packs, single sessions, or £29 written essay feedback, you can pay securely via Stripe Payment Link (with Wise or PayPal available on request).
