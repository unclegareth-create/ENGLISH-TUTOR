How a B2 thought becomes C1 / Band 7.5+ precision.

Whether it’s an essay draft or something you just said in our conversation, good feedback shows you how to express the full weight of your idea.

Before & after examples
The pattern: Rather than + V-ing ..., Subject + ought to + Verb, thereby + V-ing ...
Student draft Everyday Phrasing
“Universities should focus more on practical skills instead of just theory, because this will help graduates find jobs much faster after they leave.”
Relies on repetitive causal links (‘because this will help’) and flat clause rhythm.
How we’d improve it C1 / Band 8.0 Standard
“Rather than prioritising abstract theory, universities ought to anchor their curricula in practical training, thereby equipping graduates to navigate an increasingly competitive job market.”

Frequently asked questions.

How do lessons run, and which time zones do you teach?
Sessions run 1-to-1 online via Zoom or Google Meet with screen sharing and a shared Google Doc of notes. Based in Northern Ireland (UK time), I teach weekday and weekend slots suited to Europe, East Asia, the Middle East, and the Americas. All custom slides and official sample papers are included.
Which English levels do you accept?
My 1-to-1 coaching is built for intermediate-to-advanced adult learners (CEFR B1+ up to C2 Proficiency). Teens and younger learners can explore Chronicle ’88 and Professor West’s 3D Study in the Free Lab.
How do payment, rescheduling, and refunds work?
Starter sessions are paid at booking via Stripe; packs, single sessions, and £29 essay feedback can be paid via Stripe Payment Link, Wise, or PayPal. Any lesson can be rescheduled with 12 hours’ notice at no cost, packs are valid for 6 months, and unused pack lessons are refunded pro-rata (£75/lesson on the 5-pack; £65/lesson on the 10- and 12-packs, less any £35 starter credit applied).

Five of the 15 lessons are free in your browser. Paying £24 via Stripe unlocks all 15 lessons and the printable study guide immediately on the Course Hub and shows a backup access code for your other devices.
