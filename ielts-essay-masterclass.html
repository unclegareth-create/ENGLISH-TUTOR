1. The 5 Official IELTS Task 2 Question Types at a Glance

4-Paragraph Blueprint

Every IELTS Task 2 essay uses a clean 4-paragraph structure (Introduction → Body Paragraph 1 → Body Paragraph 2 → Conclusion, around 260–290 words). However, what goes inside Body 1 and Body 2 changes completely depending on the prompt trigger at the bottom of the question:

1. Opinion (Agree / Disagree)

"To what extent do you agree or disagree?"

  • Intro: Paraphrase + clear opinion (e.g., I largely agree because A and B).
  • Body 1: Your 1st main reason + real-world example.
  • Body 2: Your 2nd main reason (or a brief concession + rebuttal).
  • Conclusion: Restate your position clearly.
2. Discussion + Opinion

"Discuss both these views and give your own opinion."

  • Intro: Paraphrase both views + state which side you support.
  • Body 1: Discuss the side you disagree with fairly (Proponents of X argue...).
  • Body 2: Discuss the side you agree with + explain why it is stronger.
  • Conclusion: Weigh both sides and reaffirm your view.
3. Advantages Outweigh?

"Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?"

  • Intro: Paraphrase + state clearly which side is stronger (never say 50/50!).
  • Body 1: The weaker side (1–2 drawbacks/benefits acknowledged).
  • Body 2: The stronger side (2 major benefits/drawbacks showing why they outweigh).
  • Conclusion: Final judgement on why one outweighs the other.
4. Problem & Solution

"What are the causes/problems, and what solutions...?"

  • Intro: Paraphrase + preview the main cause/problem and primary solution.
  • Body 1: 1–2 root causes (or problems) with specific cause-and-effect chains.
  • Body 2: 1–2 practical solutions directly matching Body 1's causes (who does what).
  • Conclusion: Summarise cause and remedy (no agree/disagree needed!).
5. Two-Part Direct Question

"Why is this happening? Is this a positive or negative...?"

  • Intro: Paraphrase + briefly answer Question A and Question B in one thesis.
  • Body 1: Answer Question A directly and fully with an example.
  • Body 2: Answer Question B directly and fully with an example.
  • Conclusion: Combine your two answers into a concise summary.
